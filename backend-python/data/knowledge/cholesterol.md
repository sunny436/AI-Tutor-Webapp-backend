# High Cholesterol (Medoroga)

## What It Is
- Cholesterol khoon mein maujood ek chikni cheez hai. Thodi matra zaroori hai,
  par zyada hone par yeh nason ki deewaron par jam kar unhe sankra kar deti hai.
- "Bura" LDL zyada aur "achha" HDL kam hona sabse zyada nuksaan karta hai.
- Ayurveda mein isko "medoroga" aur kapha ka badhna maana jata hai, jiski jad
  dheemi agni aur "ama" (bina pacha waste) hai.
- Iske koi lakshan nahi dikhte — pata sirf blood test (lipid profile) se chalta hai.
- **Yeh page lifestyle support ke liye hai.** Agar doctor ne dawa di hai to use
  apne aap band na karein.

## Root Cause
- Din bhar baithe rehna aur exercise bilkul na hona.
- Tala hua khana, baar-baar garam kiya gaya tel, aur vanaspati/dalda.
- Packet aur bakery food (trans fat), fast food.
- Zyada cheeni aur maida — yeh triglycerides seedha badhate hain.
- Weight badhna, khaas kar pet ki charbi.
- Cigarette aur alcohol.
- Family history aur genetics.
- Diabetes, thyroid ki kami aur kidney ki problem.

## Changes To Make
- Do: roz 45 minute brisk walk ya exercise — HDL (achha cholesterol) isi se badhta hai.
- Do: khana ghar ka aur taza pakaya hua.
- Do: fibre badhayein — oats, jau, dal, sabzi, fal.
- Do: 5–10% weight kam karein agar zyada hai.
- Do: cigarette bilkul band.
- Do: doctor ke bataye time par lipid profile karwate rahein.
- Avoid: baar-baar garam kiya gaya tel — yeh sabse zyada nuksandeh hai.
- Avoid: vanaspati, dalda, margarine, bakery aur packet snacks.
- Avoid: deep-fried khana aur fast food.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** oats aur jau, alsi (flax) powder, akhrot aur badam, kaddu aur suraj-
mukhi ke beej, hari sabziyan, lauki, methi, karela, moong dal, rajma-chana,
lehsun, adrak, haldi, amla, sarson ya rice bran tel (kam matra mein), machhli,
chaas, fal (seb, amrud, anaar), green tea.

**Avoid:** vanaspati/dalda aur margarine, deep-fried khana, samosa-pakoda-puri,
bakery: cake, pastry, biscuit, cream, packet namkeen aur chips, red meat aur
organ meat, anda ka peela zyada, full-cream doodh aur malai, cheese aur butter
zyada, cheeni, mithai, cold drinks, alcohol.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: uthte hi halka garam paani, ya lehsun ki ek kali paani ke saath.
- Subah: 45 minute brisk walk ya exercise.
- Nashta: oats, daliya ya besan/moong chilla.
- Dopahar 12–2: bada meal — salad pehle, dal, sabzi, jau ya multigrain roti.
- Shaam: mutthi bhar akhrot-badam, ya green tea.
- Raat 7–8 baje: halka dinner, sabzi aur dal zyada.
- Dinner ke baad: 15 minute walk.
- Raat: 10:30 tak neend — kam neend cholesterol bigadti hai.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Surya Namaskar (roz 12 round), Trikonasana, Ardha Matsyendrasana,
  Vakrasana, Paschimottanasana, Dhanurasana, Bhujangasana, Utkatasana,
  Sarvangasana, Setu Bandhasana.
- Kapha ke liye tez aur flowing practice chahiye — dheemi restorative nahi.
- Pranayama: Kapalbhati (khali pet), Bhastrika, Anulom Vilom, Bhramari.
- Roz kam se kam 30–45 minute, hafte mein 5 din.
- High BP ya heart ki problem ho to inversions se pehle doctor se poochein.

## Home Remedies
- Lehsun ki 1–2 kali subah khali pet paani ke saath.
- Alsi (flax seed) powder 1 chammach roz.
- Oats aur jau ka regular istemaal.
- Amla juice ya taza amla roz.
- Dhaniya-jeera ka paani.
- Green tea din mein 1–2 cup.
- Roz brisk walk — yeh HDL badhane ka sabse asardar tareeka hai.
- Koi cholesterol lowering supplement, red yeast rice ya brand khud se na lein —
  aur agar doctor ki statin dawa chal rahi hai to use band na karein.

## When To See A Doctor
- Chest mein dabav, jalan ya dard — khaas kar chalte waqt ya kaam karte waqt.
  Yeh heart ka warning sign hai, turant hospital jaayein.
- Chest ka dard jo haath, gale, jabde ya peeth tak jaaye — emergency hai.
- Saans phoolna halka kaam karne par bhi.
- Chalne par pindli (calf) mein dard jo rukne par theek ho jaaye.
- Achanak ek taraf kamzori, bolne mein dikkat ya chehra tedha hona — stroke ka
  sign, turant hospital.
- Cholesterol bahut zyada ho, ya family mein kam umar mein heart attack hua ho.
- 30 ki umar ke baad har 1–2 saal mein lipid profile, sugar aur BP ka test
  karwate rahein — cholesterol ke apne koi lakshan nahi hote.
