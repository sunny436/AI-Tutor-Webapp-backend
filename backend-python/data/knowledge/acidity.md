# Acidity & Heartburn (Amlapitta)

## What It Is
- Acidity mein pet ka pitta dosha badh jata hai aur digestive acid zyada ya galat
  time par banta hai.
- Isse seene aur pet mein jalan, khatti dakar, aur khatta pani mooh tak aana
  jaisa lagta hai.
- Ayurveda isko "amlapitta" kehta hai — teekha aur garam pitta jab apni jagah se
  bahar behta hai.

## Root Cause
- Khana bahut late karna ya meal skip karna, jisse khali pet mein acid banta rahe.
- Khali pet chai ya coffee, aur din bhar zyada chai peena.
- Zyada mirchi, achaar, imli, tala hua aur bahut khatta khana.
- Khane ke turant baad let jaana ya so jaana.
- Stress, gussa, aur jaldi-jaldi khana — pitta seedha badhta hai.
- Raat ko bahut late aur bhari dinner.

## Changes To Make
- Do: har roz fix time par khana, chhote-chhote meals, aaram se chaba kar khana.
- Do: dinner 8 baje se pehle aur halka rakhein.
- Do: din bhar thoda-thoda saada paani piyein.
- Avoid: khane ke 2 ghante ke andar letna ya sona.
- Avoid: khali pet chai/coffee aur khali pet mirchi wala khana.
- Avoid: gussa ya tension mein khana khana.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** moong dal, khichdi, chawal, lauki, tori, kheera, coconut water, kela,
meethe fal (papita, anaar), thoda ghee, chaas, saunf, dhaniya, elaichi, thanda
doodh.

**Avoid:** mirchi, laal mirch, achaar, imli, vinegar, zyada tamatar, khali pet
chai/coffee, raat ko dahi, alcohol, deep-fried aur bahut namkeen packet food.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: uthte hi ek glass saada ya halka garam paani, phir 15–20 minute yoga.
- Nashta: 8–9 baje tak, halka aur garam (poha, upma, daliya).
- Dopahar: sabse bada meal 12–2 ke beech, jab digestion sabse strong hota hai.
- Shaam: 5 baje ke baad heavy chai/coffee band.
- Raat: halka dinner 8 baje se pehle, phir 10 minute slow walk.
- Sone se pehle: 10:30 tak so jaayein, screen kam karein.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Vajrasana (khane ke baad 5–10 minute), Pawanmuktasana, halka
  Bhujangasana, Ushtrasana (aaram se), Setu Bandhasana.
- Pranayama: Shitali aur Sheetkari (thandak dete hain), slow deep belly breathing,
  Anulom Vilom.
- Avoid: flare-up ke time Kapalbhati aur Bhastrika, khane ke turant baad strong
  twists aur inversions.

## Home Remedies
- Sirf kitchen ki cheezein — saunf aur dhaniya bhiga kar uska paani.
- Khane ke baad thodi saunf chabana.
- Thanda (garam nahi) doodh ya coconut water jalan ke waqt.
- Raat bhar bhige hue kishmish ya munakka subah khana.
- Kabhi bhi koi tablet, syrup, churna brand ya dosage khud se na lein.

## When To See A Doctor
- Ulti mein khoon, ya kaala/tar jaisa stool.
- Nigalne mein dikkat ya khana atakne jaisa lagna.
- Bina wajah weight kam hona ya bhookh bilkul khatam hona.
- Chest pain jo haath, gale ya jabde tak jaaye, ya saans phoolne ke saath ho —
  yeh emergency ho sakta hai, turant hospital jaayein.
- Ek mahine se zyada acidity jo in changes se bhi theek na ho.
