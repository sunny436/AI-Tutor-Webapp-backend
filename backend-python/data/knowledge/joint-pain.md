# Joint Pain & Arthritis (Sandhivata / Amavata)

## What It Is
- Ayurveda mein jodon ka dard do tarah ka hota hai:
- **Sandhivata:** jodon ka ghisna (wear and tear), umar ke saath. Dard chalne se
  badhta hai, aaram se kam hota hai. Akadan thodi der ki hoti hai.
- **Amavata:** "ama" (bina pacha waste) ka jodon mein jama hona. Subah bahut
  zyada akadan, sujan, aur kai jodon mein ek saath dard. Yeh rheumatoid arthritis
  jaisa hai aur iska proper ilaaj doctor se hona chahiye.
- Dono mein vata dosha badha hua hota hai, isliye garmi, tel aur movement teenon
  ilaaj ka hissa hain.

## Root Cause
- Umar ke saath jodon ki cartilage ka ghisna.
- Weight zyada hona — har extra kilo ghutne par 4 guna dabav dalta hai.
- Bilkul movement na hona — jod aur akad jaate hain.
- Kamzor digestion aur "ama" ka banna (Amavata mein sabse bada karan).
- Thanda, sookha, baasi khana aur thandi hawa — vata badhta hai.
- Calcium aur vitamin D ki kami.
- Purani chot ya bahut zyada physical kaam.
- Autoimmune wajah (rheumatoid), gout (uric acid), ya thyroid.

## Changes To Make
- Do: roz halki movement — jod jitna chalega utna kam dukhega.
- Do: weight kam karein agar zyada hai — ghutne ke liye sabse badi madad.
- Do: dard wali jagah par garam sikaai (sujan aur laali ho to thandi).
- Do: garam, taza, tel wala khana khaayein.
- Do: roz 15–20 minute dhoop — vitamin D ke liye.
- Avoid: thanda paani, thandi hawa aur AC seedha jodon par.
- Avoid: lambe time tak ek hi position mein baithna — har 45 minute uthein.
- Avoid: seedhi zameen par baithna, palathi maar kar lamba baithna, aur baar-baar
  seedhi (stairs) chadhna agar ghutna kharab hai.
- Avoid: bahut zyada wajan uthana aur ghutne par zor wale exercise.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** garam pakaya hua khana, ghee, til aur til tel, adrak, haldi (doodh ya
khane mein), lehsun, methi ke daane, moong dal, garam soup, hari sabziyan,
ragi aur til (calcium ke liye), badam-akhrot, alsi, machhli, dahi (din mein),
garam paani.

**Avoid:** thanda paani aur cold drinks, fridge se seedha khana, baasi aur
packet food, maida aur cheeni, tala hua, zyada namak, raat ko dahi, rajma-chana
zyada agar gas banti ho, alcohol; Amavata mein khaas kar thanda, bhaari aur
bina pacha khana. Gout ho to red meat, organ meat aur alcohol bilkul band.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: halka garam paani; methi ka bhiga paani.
- Subah: 20–30 minute halki yoga aur stretching — jodon ki akadan iski se khulti hai.
- Din mein: 15–20 minute dhoop.
- Har 45 minute: uth kar 2 minute chalein.
- Dopahar 12–2: bada meal, garam aur ghee ke saath.
- Shaam: 15–20 minute dheemi walk (surface flat ho).
- Raat: halka garam dinner 8 baje tak.
- Raat: dard wale jod par til tel ki halki maalish, phir garam sikaai.
- Hafte mein 2–3 baar poore sharir ki til tel maalish (Abhyanga).

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Tadasana, Vrikshasana (deewar ka sahara le kar), Setu Bandhasana,
  Bhujangasana, Marjariasana (cat-cow), Balasana, Supta Padangusthasana
  (patti ke saath), Viparita Karani, Shavasana.
- Ghutne ke liye khaas: leta kar seedha pair uthana (straight leg raise),
  ankle pump, aur pair ki muscles majboot karna — yeh ghutne ka bojh utha lete hain.
- Pranayama: Anulom Vilom, Bhramari, deep belly breathing.
- Roz dheere shuru karein. Dard badhe to ruk jaayein.
- **Avoid:** deep squats, Malasana aur Vajrasana agar ghutna kharab hai;
  jodon par pura wajan dalne wale poses; sujan aur laali wale flare-up mein
  koi bhi zor wala asana.

## Home Remedies
- Til ya sarson tel halka garam karke jod par 10 minute maalish, phir garam sikaai.
- Haldi wala garam doodh raat ko.
- Adrak ki chai ya adrak khane mein.
- Methi ke daane raat bhar bhigo kar subah khali pet.
- Lehsun ki 1–2 kali subah.
- Garam paani ki bottle ya garam paani se sikaai (sujan/laali ho to thandi sikaai).
- Roz dhoop aur halki walk.
- Koi painkiller ya joint supplement brand khud se lambe time tak na lein —
  painkiller kidney aur pet ko nuksaan karte hain.

## When To See A Doctor
- Jod mein sujan, laali aur garmi ke saath bukhar — infection ho sakta hai, turant.
- Chot ke baad jod ka dard, ya jod se awaaz aur uske baad wajan na sambhal paana.
- Subah 1 ghante se zyada akadan, aur kai jodon mein ek saath dard — rheumatoid
  arthritis ka test karwayein.
- Jod ka shape badalna ya ungliyan tedhi hona.
- Achanak ek jod (aksar pair ka angootha) bahut laal, garam aur bahut dardnaak —
  gout ho sakta hai, uric acid test karwayein.
- Dard ke saath bina wajah weight girna ya raat ko paseena.
- Dard jo raat ko aaram karte waqt bhi ho.
- Kamar ke dard ke saath pair mein sunnpan ya toilet control ki dikkat — turant
  hospital.
- Test karwa lein: vitamin D, calcium, uric acid, thyroid aur RA factor.
