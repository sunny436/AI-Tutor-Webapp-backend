# Migraine & Headache (Ardhavabhedaka / Shirashool)

## What It Is
- Migraine mein aksar sar ke aadhe hisse mein dhadakne wala tez dard hota hai,
  saath mein ulti jaisa lagna aur roshni/awaaz se pareshani.
- Ayurveda mein yeh mukhya roop se pitta aur vata ke bigadne se hota hai —
  isliye garmi, bhookh aur neend ki kami se badhta hai.
- Simple tension headache alag hai: yeh sar ke chaaron taraf kasav jaisa lagta
  hai aur stress se judaa hota hai.

## Root Cause
- Meal skip karna ya bahut der tak khali pet rehna — sabse aam trigger.
- Neend puri na hona ya neend ka time roz badalna.
- Dhoop, garmi aur tez roshni mein zyada rehna.
- Screen par lagatar kaam, bina break ke.
- Stress, gussa aur emotional tension.
- Zyada chai/coffee ya achanak coffee band kar dena.
- Trigger foods: cheese, chocolate, ajinomoto (MSG), packet food, alcohol.
- Ladies mein period ke aas-paas hormone badalna.
- Kabz aur kharab digestion — Ayurveda mein yeh seedha sar se juda hai.

## Changes To Make
- Do: khana kabhi skip na karein, fix time par khaayein.
- Do: roz ek hi time par soyein aur uthein — weekend par bhi.
- Do: har 30–40 minute screen ke baad 2 minute aankhein band.
- Do: apne trigger note karein (diary banayein) aur unse bachein.
- Do: dhoop mein nikalte waqt sar dhak lein.
- Avoid: bhookhe rehna, dopahar ki tez dhoop, tez awaaz aur tez roshni.
- Avoid: achanak coffee band karna — dheere dheere kam karein.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** taza pakaya hua garam khana, ghee, moong dal, khichdi, badam (bhige
hue), nariyal paani, kishmish, meethe fal (angoor, papita), doodh, dhaniya,
saunf, jeera, hari sabziyan, khoob paani.

**Avoid:** cheese aur processed cheese, chocolate, packet aur MSG wala food,
alcohol aur beer, zyada chai/coffee, bahut khatta aur fermented khana, achaar,
maida, bahut teekha aur tala hua, bahut late night khana.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: fix time par uthein, halka garam paani.
- Subah: 20 minute yoga aur breathing — pitta shaant karne ke liye.
- Nashta 9 baje tak — khali pet mat rahein.
- Dopahar: bada meal 12–2 ke beech; 12–3 tez dhoop se bachein.
- Shaam: 5 ke baad chai/coffee band.
- Raat: halka dinner 8 baje tak, phir screen kam.
- Sone se pehle: 5 minute pairon aur sar ki halki maalish; 10:30 tak so jaayein.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Balasana, Setu Bandhasana, Viparita Karani (deewar par pair),
  Shashankasana, Marjariasana, Shavasana.
- Pranayama: Shitali aur Sheetkari (sar ki garmi nikalte hain), Bhramari
  (gunguna kar — migraine mein sabse madadgar), Anulom Vilom.
- Attack ke waqt: sirf Shavasana aur Bhramari, andhere shaant kamre mein.
- Avoid: attack ke waqt Kapalbhati, Bhastrika, Sirsasana aur koi bhi inversion.

## Home Remedies
- Nariyal ya til ke tel se sar aur pairon ki halki maalish, raat ko.
- Maathe par thanda geela kapda, andhere kamre mein.
- Adrak wali halki chai (agar pitta type na ho) ya dhaniya ka paani.
- 5–6 bhige badam roz subah.
- Dhaniya beej ka paani — pitta ko thanda karta hai.
- Attack shuru hote hi turant paani piyein aur kuch halka khaayein.
- Koi painkiller tablet ya brand khud se aadat na banayein.

## When To See A Doctor
- Zindagi ka sabse tez sar dard, jo achanak aaya ho — turant hospital.
- Sar dard ke saath bukhar, gardan akadna, ya sharir mein daane.
- Bolne, dekhne, chalne mein dikkat, ya haath-pair mein kamzori/sunnpan.
- Sar par chot lagne ke baad ka sar dard.
- Sar dard jo lagatar badhta ja raha ho, ya subah uthte hi ulti ke saath ho.
- Hafte mein 2 se zyada baar migraine, ya jo in changes se na sudhre — doctor se
  proper diagnosis karwayein.
