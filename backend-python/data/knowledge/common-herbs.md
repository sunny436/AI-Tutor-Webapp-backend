# Common Ayurvedic Herbs (Ashwagandha, Triphala, Brahmi, Tulsi, Amla, Giloy)

## What It Is
- **Ashwagandha** — jad wali jadi buti. Stress, thakan aur kamzori ke liye
  Ayurveda mein sabse zyada istemaal hoti hai. Vata aur kapha ko shaant karti hai.
- **Triphala** — teen fal ka mel: amla, haritaki (harad) aur bibhitaki (baheda).
  Pet saaf rakhne aur digestion ke liye.
- **Brahmi** — dimaag ki jadi buti. Yaadasht, dhyan aur mann ki shaanti ke liye.
- **Tulsi** — ghar ka sabse aam paudha. Sardi-khaansi, saans aur immunity ke liye.
- **Amla** — vitamin C ka sabse achha natural source. Baal, skin, aankh aur
  immunity, teenon ke liye.
- **Giloy (Guduchi)** — bel jaisi jadi buti. Immunity aur baar-baar bukhar ke
  liye istemaal hoti hai.

## Root Cause
- Ayurveda mein har jadi buti kisi dosha ke bigadne par di jaati hai, sabko nahi.
- **Vata bigda ho** (sookhapan, thakan, ghabrahat, neend na aana): ashwagandha,
  brahmi.
- **Pitta bigda ho** (garmi, acidity, jalan, muhase): amla, giloy, brahmi.
- **Kapha bigda ho** (bhaaripan, susti, balgam, weight): triphala, tulsi.
- Isliye "sabke liye ek hi herb" wali soch galat hai — jo ek ke liye achha hai
  wo doosre ke liye ulta pad sakta hai.

## Changes To Make
- Do: ek waqt par ek hi jadi buti se shuruaat karein, taaki pata chale kya suit
  kar raha hai.
- Do: pehle khana, neend aur routine theek karein — jadi buti unki jagah nahi leti.
- Do: taza aur bharosemand quality lein.
- Do: pregnancy, breastfeeding, ya koi dawa chal rahi ho to pehle doctor se poochein.
- Avoid: kai herbs ek saath, lambe time tak, bina kisi salah ke.
- Avoid: online "ayurvedic" capsule aur powder jinme kya mila hai pata na ho —
  kai mein heavy metal aur steroid mile hote hain.
- Avoid: apni chal rahi dawa (BP, sugar, thyroid) inke bharose band karna.

## Food Chart
**Eat (khaane ka aam tareeka):** amla — taza fal, juice ya subah khali pet.
Tulsi — 4–5 patte subah, ya chai mein. Ashwagandha — raat ko garam doodh ke
saath. Triphala — raat ko garam paani ke saath. Brahmi — subah doodh ya paani
ke saath. Giloy — uska paani ya kadha, mausam badalne par.

**Avoid:** khali pet bahut garam herbs (ashwagandha, giloy) agar acidity rehti
ho. Amla ko doodh ke saath nahi. Bahut zyada matra — jyada lene se fayda nahi,
nuksaan hota hai. Alcohol ke saath koi bhi herb.

## Daily Routine
- Subah khali pet: amla (taza ya juice), ya tulsi ke 4–5 patte.
- Subah: brahmi, agar padhai ya dhyan ke liye le rahe hain.
- Din mein: tulsi-adrak ki chai.
- Mausam badalne par: giloy ka paani, kuch dino ke liye — poore saal nahi.
- Raat khane ke baad: ashwagandha garam doodh ke saath (neend aur thakan ke liye).
- Sone se pehle: triphala garam paani ke saath (agar kabz rehti ho).
- Ek herb ko 4–6 hafte lein, phir break — Ayurveda mein bhi lagatar chalana
  sahi nahi maana jata.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Jadi buti akeli kaam nahi karti — yoga aur pranayama ke saath hi asli fayda hai.
- Ashwagandha ke saath: Shavasana, Yoga Nidra, Anulom Vilom (stress ke liye).
- Brahmi ke saath: Bhramari, meditation, Sarvangasana (dimaag ke liye).
- Triphala ke saath: Pawanmuktasana, Malasana, Bhujangasana (pet ke liye).
- Tulsi aur giloy ke saath: Kapalbhati, Bhastrika, Ujjayi (saans aur immunity).
- Amla ke saath: Surya Namaskar aur Sarvangasana (skin aur baal ke liye).

## Home Remedies
- **Amla:** roz 1 taza amla, ya 20 ml juice paani ke saath subah.
- **Tulsi:** 4–5 patte subah khali pet chabana, ya adrak-kali mirch ke saath chai.
- **Ashwagandha:** raat ko garam doodh mein, thakan aur neend ke liye.
- **Triphala:** raat ko garam paani ke saath, kabz ke liye.
- **Brahmi:** subah doodh ke saath, ya brahmi ke patte.
- **Giloy:** uske tane ka paani ubaal kar, mausam badalne par kuch din.
- **Kisi bhi herb ki matra, brand ya dosage yahan nahi di ja sakti** — yeh umar,
  halat aur chal rahi dawa par nirbhar karta hai, aur ise Ayurveda doctor
  (Vaidya) hi decide kar sakta hai.

## When To See A Doctor
- **Ashwagandha na lein** agar: pregnancy ho, thyroid tez ho (hyperthyroid), ya
  koi autoimmune bimari ho — yeh use badha sakti hai.
- **Giloy na lein** lambe time tak — kuch logon mein liver ko nuksaan ke mamle
  saamne aaye hain. Autoimmune bimari mein bhi bachein.
- **Triphala na lein** pregnancy mein, ya agar loose motions rehte hon.
- **Amla dhyan se** agar khoon patla karne ki dawa chal rahi ho.
- **Brahmi** thyroid ki dawa ka asar badal sakti hai.
- Koi bhi herb lene ke baad daane, khujli, ulti, pet dard ya peeli aankhein —
  turant band karein aur doctor ko dikhayein.
- Sugar, BP ya thyroid ki dawa chal rahi ho to koi bhi herb shuru karne se pehle
  doctor ko zaroor batayein — yeh dawa ka asar badha ya ghata sakti hain.
- Bachchon aur pregnancy mein koi bhi jadi buti bina doctor ki salah ke nahi.
- Yaad rakhein: "natural" ka matlab "hamesha safe" nahi hota.
