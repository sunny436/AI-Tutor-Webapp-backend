# Fatigue & Weakness (Daurbalya / Klama)

## What It Is
- Lagatar thakan matlab: aaram karne ke baad bhi taazgi na aana, aur roz ke kaam
  bhi bhaari lagna.
- Ayurveda mein iski jad kamzor "ojas" (body ki asli taakat) aur kamzor agni hai —
  khana jaata hai par taakat nahi banti.
- Bahut zaroori: lagatar thakan ke peeche aksar ek asli medical wajah hoti hai
  (khoon ki kami, thyroid, sugar, vitamin ki kami). Isliye pehla kadam blood
  test hai, ghar ke upay nahi.

## Root Cause
- Khoon ki kami (anaemia) — sabse aam wajah, khaas kar ladies mein.
- Vitamin D aur B12 ki kami.
- Thyroid ki kami.
- Diabetes ya sugar ka bigda hona.
- Neend puri na hona ya neend ki quality kharab hona.
- Lagatar stress aur mansik dabav.
- Khana skip karna, crash diet, ya protein bilkul kam khana.
- Paani kam peena.
- Bilkul exercise na hona (isse thakan badhti hai, kam nahi).
- Bahut zyada chai/coffee aur cheeni — energy upar-neeche hoti rehti hai.
- Purani bimari, infection, ya koi dawa ka side effect.

## Changes To Make
- Do: sabse pehle blood test karwayein — Hb, thyroid, sugar, vitamin D aur B12.
- Do: khana kabhi skip na karein, har meal mein protein rakhein.
- Do: roz 30 minute walk — thakan mein movement dawa ka kaam karti hai.
- Do: 7–8 ghante neend, fix time par.
- Do: roz 15–20 minute dhoop.
- Do: din bhar 8–10 glass paani.
- Avoid: cheeni aur cold drink se energy lena — 1 ghante baad aur zyada thakan hoti hai.
- Avoid: din mein lambi jhapki (20 minute se zyada nahi).
- Avoid: raat ko der tak jagna aur screen.
- Avoid: bahut zyada chai/coffee.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** khajoor, kishmish aur anjeer (bhige hue), badam-akhrot, moong aur
masoor dal, rajma-chana, anda, paneer, dahi, doodh, palak aur hari sabziyan
(iron), chukandar aur anaar, amla aur nimbu (iron absorb karne ke liye), ghee,
oats aur ragi, kela, shakarkandi, til-gud ke laddu, ashwagandha wala doodh.

**Avoid:** khana skip karna, crash diet, cheeni aur mithai, cold drinks aur
energy drinks, maida aur packet food, bahut zyada chai/coffee (khaas kar khane
ke turant baad — iron absorb nahi hota), alcohol aur cigarette.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: fix time par uthein, halka garam paani.
- Subah: bhige badam, khajoor aur kishmish khali pet.
- Subah: 20–30 minute yoga ya walk (dheere shuru karein).
- Nashta 8–9 baje: bhaari aur protein wala — chilla, anda, poha, doodh.
- Din mein: 15–20 minute dhoop.
- Dopahar 12–2: bada meal — dal, sabzi, roti, dahi, salad.
- 4–5 baje: nuts, khajoor ya fal ka snack.
- Raat 8 baje tak: halka dinner.
- Raat: pairon ki til tel maalish, screen band, 10:30 tak neend.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Surya Namaskar (dheere, 4–6 round se shuru), Tadasana, Bhujangasana,
  Setu Bandhasana, Viparita Karani, Balasana, Ustrasana, Shavasana.
- Roz 10 minute Yoga Nidra — yeh ek ghante ki neend jitna aaram deta hai.
- Pranayama: Bhastrika aur Kapalbhati (energy ke liye, khali pet, dheere shuru),
  Anulom Vilom, Surya Bhedana, Bhramari.
- Shuruaat chhoti rakhein — 10 minute bhi kaafi hai. Roz karna zaroori hai.
- Thakan mein bilkul aaram karna galat hai; halki movement hi energy laati hai.

## Home Remedies
- Ashwagandha wala garam doodh raat ko — Ayurveda mein taakat ke liye sabse khaas.
- Roz 5–6 bhige badam aur 2 khajoor subah khali pet.
- Kishmish raat bhar bhigo kar, subah paani ke saath (iron ke liye).
- Chukandar aur anaar ka juice.
- Amla juice ya taza amla — iron absorb karne mein madad karta hai.
- Til-gud ke laddu.
- Roz dhoop aur walk.
- Chai/coffee khane ke turant baad na piyein — iron absorb nahi hota.
- Koi multivitamin, tonic ya energy supplement brand khud se na lein — pehle test
  karwayein, phir doctor jo kami hai wahi theek karega.

## When To See A Doctor
- Thakan jo 2–3 hafte se lagatar ho aur aaram se bhi na jaaye.
- Bahut peela dikhna, chakkar, dil tez dhadakna ya halka kaam karne par bhi
  saans phoolna — khoon ki kami ho sakti hai.
- Bina wajah weight girna.
- Bukhar, raat ko paseena, ya gaanth (lymph node) mehsoos hona.
- Chest pain ya saans phoolna — turant.
- Bahut pyaas, baar-baar urine aur bahut bhookh — sugar ka test.
- Thand lagna, weight badhna aur baal jhadna — thyroid ka test.
- Ladies mein bahut bhaari period ke saath thakan.
- Thakan ke saath lagatar udasi ya jeene ka mann na hona.
- Kaale stool ya stool/urine mein khoon.
- **Sabse zaroori:** yeh test karwa lein — Hb aur CBC, thyroid (TSH), sugar
  (HbA1c), vitamin D aur B12. Lagatar thakan ki lagbhag hamesha koi ek asli
  wajah hoti hai jo test se pakdi jaati hai aur theek ho sakti hai.
