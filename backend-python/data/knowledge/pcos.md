# PCOS / PCOD (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome)

## What It Is
- PCOS ek hormonal condition hai jisme ovary theek se egg release nahi karti,
  isliye periods irregular ho jaate hain.
- Saath mein aksar weight badhna, chehre par baal, muhase, aur sar ke baal
  patle hona dikhta hai.
- Iske peeche aksar insulin resistance hota hai — body insulin ka theek istemaal
  nahi kar paati.
- Ayurveda mein isko kapha ka badhna aur "artava" (period) ke rasta ka rukna
  maana jata hai, jiski jad kamzor digestion aur inactive lifestyle hai.
- Yeh page lifestyle support ke liye hai. PCOS ki proper jaanch aur ilaaj
  gynaecologist se hi karwana zaroori hai.

## Root Cause
- Insulin resistance — sabse badi wajah, aur yahi weight aur hormone dono bigadta hai.
- Din bhar baithe rehna, bilkul exercise na hona.
- Refined carbs: maida, cheeni, cold drink, bakery aur packet food.
- Neend puri na hona aur raat ko der tak jagna.
- Lagatar stress — cortisol hormone ko aur bigadta hai.
- Weight badhna, jo phir aur zyada hormone imbalance karta hai.
- Family history (genetics) bhi role karti hai.

## Changes To Make
- Do: roz 40–45 minute exercise — walk, strength training ya yoga. PCOS mein
  movement dawa jitna hi zaroori hai.
- Do: 5–10% weight kam karne se hi periods aksar wapas regular hone lagte hain.
- Do: har meal mein protein aur fibre — sugar spike rukta hai.
- Do: raat 10:30 tak sona, 7–8 ghante neend.
- Do: har 3–4 ghante mein khaayein, khana skip na karein.
- Avoid: cheeni, cold drinks, packet juice, maida, bakery aur fast food.
- Avoid: crash diet aur bhookhe rehna — isse hormone aur bigadta hai.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** hari sabziyan, moong aur masoor dal, rajma-chana (achhe se pakaya),
paneer, anda, machhli, badam-akhrot, alsi aur kaddu ke beej, jau (barley),
bajra, oats, methi ke daane (bhige hue), amla, dalchini, haldi, chaas,
kam meethe fal (jamun, amrud, anaar).

**Avoid:** cheeni aur meethe, cold drinks aur packet juice, maida, white bread,
biscuit-cake, fast food aur fried, zyada chawal aur aloo ek saath, packet
processed food, alcohol, zyada dairy agar muhase badhein.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: uthte hi methi ka bhiga paani ya halka garam paani.
- Subah: 40 minute exercise — brisk walk, strength ya yoga.
- Nashta: protein wala (anda, paneer, moong chilla) — cereal/bread nahi.
- Dopahar: bada meal 12–2 ke beech, dal + sabzi + thodi roti.
- Shaam: nuts ya chaas ka chhota snack.
- Raat: halka dinner 8 baje tak, sabzi aur protein zyada.
- Raat: screen band, 10:30 tak neend.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Surya Namaskar (roz 8–12 round), Baddha Konasana (butterfly),
  Supta Baddha Konasana, Bhujangasana, Dhanurasana, Malasana, Setu Bandhasana,
  Ustrasana, Balasana.
- Pelvic aur pet ke aas-paas blood flow badhane wale asanas sabse zyada madad karte hain.
- Pranayama: Kapalbhati (khali pet), Bhastrika, Anulom Vilom, Bhramari.
- Roz 10 minute meditation — stress hormone PCOS ka bada driver hai.
- Avoid: period ke bhaari din mein strong inversions aur pet par dabav wale asanas.

## Home Remedies
- Methi ke daane raat bhar bhigo kar subah khali pet paani ke saath.
- Dalchini (cinnamon) ka paani ya khane mein — insulin ke liye madadgar.
- Amla ka juice ya taza amla roz.
- Alsi aur kaddu ke beej roz ek chammach.
- Haldi wala garam doodh.
- Roz walk — sabse asardar "remedy" yahi hai.
- Koi hormone tablet, supplement brand ya dosage khud se na lein.

## When To See A Doctor
- Period 3 mahine se zyada band ho jaayein.
- Bahut zyada bleeding, ya bleeding jo 7 din se zyada chale.
- Bachche ke liye 1 saal se koshish kar rahi hon aur na ho raha ho.
- Chehre par tezi se baal badhna, awaaz bhaari hona, ya bahut tez baal jhadna.
- Gardan ya bagal ki skin kaali padna (insulin resistance ka sign).
- Bahut tez pet ya pelvic dard.
- PCOS ki confirm jaanch (ultrasound, hormone aur sugar test) gynaecologist se
  zaroor karwayein — yeh lifestyle steps unke ilaaj ke saath chalte hain, uski
  jagah nahi lete.
