# Low Mood & Sadness (Avasada)

## What It Is
- Lagatar udasi, kisi cheez mein mann na lagna, aur energy bilkul kam mehsoos hona.
- Ayurveda mein isko aksar badhe hue kapha (bhaaripan, susti) aur bigde hue vata
  (khaali-khaali lagna) ka mel maana jata hai.
- Yeh sirf "kamzor mann" nahi hai — yeh ek asli health condition hai jise sahi
  madad se behtar kiya ja sakta hai.
- Yeh page lifestyle support ke liye hai. Yeh doctor ya counsellor ke ilaaj ki
  jagah bilkul nahi leta.

## Root Cause
- Neend ka bigda hua pattern — bahut zyada ya bahut kam sona.
- Din bhar bilkul movement na hona aur dhoop na milna.
- Akelapan aur logon se kat jaana.
- Lagatar stress, ya koi bada nuksaan/badlav.
- Bahut zyada screen aur social media.
- Kharab digestion aur bhaari, processed khana.
- Nasha ya alcohol.
- Kabhi kabhi thyroid, khoon ki kami ya vitamin D/B12 ki kami bhi wajah hoti hai —
  isliye test karwana zaroori hai.

## Changes To Make
- Do: roz subah 15–20 minute dhoop mein rahein — sabse simple aur asardar step.
- Do: roz thoda movement, chahe 15 minute walk hi ho.
- Do: sona aur uthna fix time par.
- Do: din mein ek chhota kaam poora karein — chhoti jeet mood banati hai.
- Do: roz kam se kam ek insaan se baat karein.
- Avoid: din bhar bistar par rehna aur akele band rehna.
- Avoid: alcohol aur nasha — yeh mood ko aur neeche le jaate hain.
- Avoid: raat ko lambi social media scrolling.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** garam taza pakaya hua khana, ghee, doodh, bhige badam aur akhrot,
khajoor, kela, anaar, oats, moong dal, khichdi, haldi, adrak, tulsi ki chai,
hari sabziyan, alsi aur kaddu ke beej.

**Avoid:** alcohol, packet aur processed food, bahut zyada meetha aur maida,
bahut zyada chai/coffee, thanda-baasi khana, bhari fried khana, khana skip
karna.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: fix time par uthein, chahe mann na kare — pardah kholein, roshni aane dein.
- Subah: 15–20 minute dhoop mein walk.
- Subah: 20 minute yoga aur pranayama.
- Nashta time par, garam aur halka.
- Din: ek chhota kaam chunein aur poora karein.
- Shaam: kisi se milna ya baat karna, koi hobby.
- Raat: halka dinner 8 baje tak, screen band 1 ghanta pehle.
- Raat: til tel se pairon ki maalish, 10:30 tak so jaayein.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Surya Namaskar (dheere, jitna ho sake), Bhujangasana, Ustrasana,
  Setu Bandhasana, Matsyasana, Tadasana, Shavasana.
- Chest kholne wale (backbend) asanas kapha aur bhaaripan ke liye khaas achhe hain.
- Pranayama: Bhastrika aur Kapalbhati (energy ke liye, khali pet), Anulom Vilom,
  Bhramari, Surya Bhedana.
- Roz 10 minute Yoga Nidra ya simple meditation.
- Shuruaat chhoti rakhein — 10 minute bhi kaafi hai, roz karna zaroori hai.

## Home Remedies
- Roz subah dhoop — vitamin D aur body clock dono theek karta hai.
- Til ya nariyal tel se sar aur pairon ki maalish.
- Garam doodh mein haldi ya kesar, raat ko.
- Tulsi ya brahmi ki chai.
- Bhige badam aur akhrot roz.
- Ek gratitude diary — roz 3 achhi cheezein likhein.
- Koi antidepressant ya koi bhi tablet/brand khud se na lein aur na hi band karein.

## When To See A Doctor
- Khud ko nuksaan pahunchane ya jeene ka mann na hone ke vichar — turant kisi ko
  batayein aur bina der doctor/helpline se sampark karein. Yeh emergency hai.
- Udasi jo 2 hafte se zyada lagatar chal rahi ho.
- Roz ka kaam, job, padhai ya rishte prabhavit ho rahe hon.
- Khana ya neend bilkul bigad gaya ho, ya weight tezi se badla ho.
- Nashe ya alcohol par nirbharta badh rahi ho.
- Thyroid, khoon ki kami aur vitamin D/B12 ka test zaroor karwayein — inki kami
  bilkul aise hi lakshan deti hai.
