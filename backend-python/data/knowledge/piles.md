# Piles / Haemorrhoids (Arsha)

## What It Is
- Piles mein anus ke aas-paas ki nasein soojh jaati hain aur wahan gaanth
  (masse) jaisi ban jaati hai.
- Zor lagane se dard, jalan aur kabhi kabhi stool ke saath khoon aata hai.
- Ayurveda isko "arsha" kehta hai, aur iski jad lagbhag hamesha lambi chalne
  wali kabz aur kamzor digestion hoti hai.

## Root Cause
- Purani kabz aur toilet mein baar-baar zor lagana.
- Toilet par bahut der tak baithna (phone ke saath).
- Fibre kam aur paani kam — stool sakht ho jata hai.
- Bahut teekha, tala hua aur masaledar khana.
- Din bhar ek jagah baithe rehna, khaas kar hard seat par.
- Zyada bhaari wajan uthana.
- Pregnancy aur delivery ke baad ka pressure.
- Motapa aur bilkul physical activity na hona.

## Changes To Make
- Do: kabz ko theek karna sabse pehla kaam hai — paani aur fibre badhayein.
- Do: toilet mein 5 minute se zyada mat baithein, phone andar mat le jaayein.
- Do: urge aate hi jaayein, roken nahi.
- Do: roz walk karein, har ghante seat se uthein.
- Do: motion ke baad saada paani se saaf karein, rough paper se ragdein nahi.
- Avoid: zor lagana, bhaari wajan uthana.
- Avoid: mirchi, achaar, tala hua aur bahut masaledar khana.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** papita, paka kela, amrud, bhige anjeer aur munakka, palak aur hari
sabziyan, daliya, oats, moong dal, chaas, lauki, ghee ke saath garam khana,
alsi powder, din bhar 8–10 glass paani.

**Avoid:** mirchi, laal mirch, achaar, tala hua khana, maida aur biscuit,
non-veg jo bhaari ho, alcohol, zyada chai/coffee, bahut namkeen packet food,
raat ko heavy khana.

## Daily Routine
- Subah uthte hi: 2 glass halka garam paani.
- Subah: fix time par toilet, bina zor aur bina jaldbaazi ke.
- Subah: 20 minute halki yoga ya walk.
- Din bhar: har ghante uth kar 2 minute chalein.
- Shaam: bhige hue munakka ya anjeer.
- Raat: dinner 8 baje se pehle, halka. Sone se pehle garam doodh mein 1 chammach ghee.
- Dard ya sujan ke waqt: garam paani ke tub mein 10 minute baithna (sitz bath).

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Malasana, Balasana, Viparita Karani (deewar par pair), Pawanmuktasana,
  Sarvangasana (sirf agar comfortable ho), Ashwini Mudra (anus ko halke se
  sikodna-chhodna).
- Pranayama: Anulom Vilom, slow deep belly breathing.
- Avoid: bleeding ya tez dard ke time Kapalbhati, Bhastrika, heavy squats aur
  weight lifting. Pehle doctor se poochein.

## Home Remedies
- Sitz bath: garam paani ke tub mein 10–15 minute baithna, din mein 2 baar.
- Chaas mein bhuna jeera aur chutki sendha namak.
- Bhige hue munakka/anjeer roz subah.
- Raat ko garam doodh ke saath 1 chammach ghee — stool naram rakhta hai.
- Papita aur paka kela roz.
- Koi piles cream, tablet, churna brand ya dosage khud se na lagayein ya lein.

## When To See A Doctor
- Bahut zyada khoon aana, ya har motion ke saath khoon.
- Anus ke bahar aayi gaanth jo wapas andar na jaaye, ya bahut dard kare.
- Chakkar, kamzori ya bahut peela dikhna (khoon ki kami ke signs).
- Bina wajah weight kam hona ya stool ki aadat achanak badal jaana.
- Bukhar ke saath anus mein sujan aur tez dard (infection ho sakta hai).
- Koi bhi rectal bleeding pehli baar ho to doctor se check karwana zaroori hai —
  yeh piles ke alawa bhi kuch ho sakta hai.
