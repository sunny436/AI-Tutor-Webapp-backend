# Period Pain & Cramps (Kashtartava)

## What It Is
- Period ke time pet ke neeche, kamar aur jaangon mein marod jaisa dard hona.
- Ayurveda mein iski jad vata dosha ka pelvic area mein ruk jaana hai — isliye
  garmi, tel aur aaram sabse zyada madad karte hain.
- Halka dard normal hai. Lekin dard jo kaam rok de, wo normal nahi hai aur uski
  jaanch honi chahiye.

## Root Cause
- Vata ka badhna: thanda khana, thandi hawa, irregular routine.
- Kabz — pet bhara hone se pelvic pressure badhta hai.
- Stress aur neend ki kami.
- Bilkul physical activity na hona — pelvic blood flow kam ho jaata hai.
- Thanda paani aur cold drinks, khaas kar period ke aas-paas.
- Khoon ki kami aur poshan ki kami.
- Kabhi kabhi endometriosis, fibroid ya infection — inka dard zyada tez hota hai
  aur inki proper jaanch zaroori hai.

## Changes To Make
- Do: pet aur kamar par garam paani ki bottle ya heating pad rakhein.
- Do: period ke dino mein garam paani hi piyein.
- Do: halki walk aur stretching karein — bistar par bilkul pade rehna dard badhata hai.
- Do: period se 1 hafta pehle se khana halka aur garam rakhein.
- Do: kabz ko theek rakhein.
- Avoid: thanda paani, cold drinks aur ice cream period ke aas-paas.
- Avoid: bahut teekha, tala hua aur packet food.
- Avoid: period ke dino mein intense workout aur bhaari wajan uthana.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** garam pakaya hua khana, khichdi, moong dal, ghee, til aur gud,
khajoor, adrak ki chai, saunf aur ajwain ka paani, haldi wala doodh, palak
aur hari sabziyan, anaar, chukandar, kela, badam-akhrot, garam soup.

**Avoid:** thanda paani aur cold drinks, ice cream, packet aur processed food,
zyada cheeni aur maida, bahut zyada chai/coffee, alcohol, bahut namkeen food
(sujan badhata hai), raw salad aur thanda khana in dino mein.

## Daily Routine
- Period se 1 hafta pehle: khana halka aur garam, neend jaldi, exercise halki.
- Period ke dino mein subah: garam paani, ajwain ya saunf ki chai.
- Dopahar: garam simple khana — khichdi ya dal-chawal.
- Din bhar: garam paani ki bottle pet/kamar par jab dard ho.
- Shaam: 15 minute dheemi walk ya halki stretching.
- Raat: pet aur kamar par til tel ki halki maalish, phir garam sikaai.
- Raat: jaldi sona, aaram zyada.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Supta Baddha Konasana, Balasana, Malasana, Cat-Cow (Marjariasana),
  Janu Sirsasana, Setu Bandhasana (halka), Viparita Karani, Shavasana.
- Yeh sab pelvic muscles ko dheela karte hain aur blood flow badhate hain.
- Pranayama: slow deep belly breathing, Anulom Vilom, Bhramari.
- Baaki dino mein: Surya Namaskar aur regular yoga — isse agle period ka dard
  kam hota hai. Asli fayda regular practice se milta hai, sirf period ke din nahi.
- Avoid: period ke bhaari din mein Sirsasana, Sarvangasana aur pet par dabav
  wale strong asanas.

## Home Remedies
- Garam paani ki bottle pet aur kamar par — sabse simple aur asardar.
- Adrak ki chai ya adrak-gud ka paani.
- Ajwain ya saunf garam paani mein.
- Til tel se pet aur kamar ki halki maalish, phir garam sikaai.
- Haldi wala garam doodh raat ko.
- Til-gud ka laddu period se pehle ke hafte mein.
- Koi painkiller tablet ya brand khud se aadat na banayein — baar-baar zaroorat
  pade to doctor se milein.

## When To See A Doctor
- Dard itna ki school, college ya job chhodni pade, ya painkiller se bhi na jaaye.
- Dard jo har mahine badhta ja raha ho.
- Bahut zyada bleeding — har 1–2 ghante mein pad badalna, ya bade clots.
- Period ke alawa dino mein bhi pelvic dard, ya sex ke waqt dard.
- Bukhar ke saath pelvic dard aur badbudar discharge (infection ho sakta hai).
- Bachche ke liye 1 saal se koshish kar rahi hon.
- Dard jo pehle nahi tha aur achanak shuru ho gaya ho.
- Endometriosis, fibroid aur PCOS jaisi condition ka proper diagnosis sirf
  gynaecologist hi kar sakta hai — inko sirf "normal period pain" maan kar mat
  chhodein.
