# Back Pain (Katishoola / Gridhrasi)

## What It Is
- Kamar dard aksar muscles ke kheenchne, galat posture, ya kamzor core ki wajah
  se hota hai — 90% mamlon mein koi gambhir bimari nahi hoti.
- Agar dard kamar se neeche pair tak jaaye, saath mein jhunjhuni ya sunnpan ho,
  to isko Ayurveda "gridhrasi" (sciatica) kehta hai — nas par dabav.
- Ayurveda mein iski jad badha hua vata hai: sookhapan, thand aur akadan.
  Isliye garmi, tel aur halki movement sabse zyada madad karte hain.

## Root Cause
- Galat posture — jhuk kar baithna, phone dekhte hue gardan/kamar mod'na.
- Ghanton tak ek hi jagah baithna, khaas kar kursi par bina support ke.
- Core aur kamar ki muscles ka kamzor hona.
- Bhaari wajan galat tarike se uthana (kamar se jhuk kar, ghutne se nahi).
- Weight zyada hona, khaas kar pet ka.
- Bahut naram gadda ya galat takiya.
- Thandi hawa, AC seedha kamar par.
- Neend aur stress ki kami — muscles kas jaati hain.
- Kabz aur gas — kamar par dabav badhta hai.
- Ladies mein period, pregnancy aur delivery ke baad.

## Changes To Make
- Do: har 30–45 minute baad uth kar 2 minute chalein.
- Do: kursi par kamar seedhi, pair zameen par, screen aankh ki height par.
- Do: wajan uthate waqt ghutne modein, kamar nahi.
- Do: garam sikaai roz — dard aur akadan dono kam hote hain.
- Do: core aur kamar ki muscles majboot karein — yeh asli permanent ilaaj hai.
- Do: gadda medium-firm rakhein; peeth ke bal soyein to ghutne ke neeche takiya,
  karwat lein to ghutnon ke beech takiya.
- Avoid: bed rest lambe time tak — 1–2 din se zyada aaram dard badhata hai.
- Avoid: kamar se jhuk kar wajan uthana, aur ek taraf bhaari bag latkana.
- Avoid: bahut naram sofa aur gadda.
- Avoid: seedha AC ya thandi hawa kamar par.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** garam pakaya hua khana, ghee, til aur til tel, haldi (doodh mein),
adrak, lehsun, methi, moong dal, ragi aur til (calcium), badam-akhrot, alsi,
hari sabziyan, garam soup, dahi (din mein), garam paani, khoob paani.

**Avoid:** thanda paani aur cold drinks, fridge se seedha khana, baasi aur
packet food, maida aur cheeni, tala hua khana, zyada namak (sujan badhata hai),
gas banane wale khane agar kabz rehti ho, alcohol aur cigarette (disc ki sehat
kharab karte hain).

## Daily Routine
- Subah: halka garam paani; bistar se karwat le kar uthein, seedha jhatke se nahi.
- Subah: 20–30 minute halki yoga aur stretching.
- Din bhar: har 30–45 minute uth kar chalein.
- Baithne mein: kamar ke peeche chhota takiya ya rolled towel.
- Dopahar 12–2: bada meal, garam.
- Shaam: 20 minute dheemi walk — flat surface par.
- Raat: halka garam dinner 8 baje tak.
- Raat: kamar par til tel ki halki maalish, phir 10–15 minute garam sikaai.
- Hafte mein 2–3 baar poore sharir ki til tel maalish.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Marjariasana (cat-cow), Balasana, Bhujangasana (halka), Setu
  Bandhasana, Supta Padangusthasana (patti ke saath), Pawanmuktasana,
  Adho Mukha Svanasana, Shalabhasana (halka), Shavasana.
- Core ke liye: pet ke bal leta kar halka Shalabhasana, aur peeth ke bal
  leta kar pet andar kheench kar 10 second rokna.
- Pranayama: Anulom Vilom, Bhramari, deep belly breathing.
- Roz 20 minute, dheere aur bina dard ke. Dard badhe to turant ruk jaayein.
- **Avoid:** aage jhuk kar pair choona (Padahastasana) agar disc ki problem ho;
  tez twists, Halasana, Sirsasana; acute dard mein koi bhi zor wala asana.
  Slip disc ya sciatica hai to physiotherapist ya doctor se poochh kar hi karein.

## Home Remedies
- Til ya sarson tel halka garam karke kamar par 10 minute halki maalish.
- Uske baad garam paani ki bottle se 10–15 minute sikaai.
- Haldi wala garam doodh raat ko.
- Adrak ki chai.
- Methi ke daane bhigo kar subah.
- Naye dard (48 ghante ke andar) mein pehle thandi sikaai, phir garam.
- Roz halki walk — bistar par pade rehna sabse bada nuksaan karta hai.
- Koi painkiller lambe time tak khud se na lein — kidney aur pet ko nuksaan hota hai.

## When To See A Doctor
- **Turant hospital jaayein agar:** toilet ya urine ka control chala jaaye, ya
  jaangon ke beech sunnpan ho — yeh nas par gambhir dabav ka sign hai.
- Dono pairon mein kamzori ya sunnpan.
- Kamar dard ke saath bukhar, ya bina wajah weight girna.
- Girne ya chot lagne ke baad ka kamar dard.
- Dard jo pair mein ghutne se neeche tak jaaye aur badhta jaaye.
- Raat ko aaram karte waqt bhi dard, ya dard se neend khul jaana.
- Pair ghaseetne lage ya chappal pair se girne lage.
- 6 hafte se zyada dard jo in upayon se na sudhre.
- 50 ki umar ke baad naya kamar dard, ya cancer/osteoporosis ki history ho.
- Test karwa lein: vitamin D, calcium aur zaroorat par X-ray/MRI — yeh doctor
  hi decide karega.
