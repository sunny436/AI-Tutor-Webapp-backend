# Constipation (Vibandha)

## What It Is
- Kabz mein vata dosha aant (intestine) ko sukha deta hai, jisse stool sakht ho
  jata hai aur aage nahi badhta.
- Roz pet saaf na hona, zor lagana, aur pet bhara-bhara lagna iske signs hain.
- Ayurveda mein iska naam "vibandha" hai — apan vayu ka neeche ki taraf na behna.

## Root Cause
- Paani bahut kam peena — sabse aam wajah.
- Fibre kam khana: maida, packet food, biscuit, refined cheezein.
- Din bhar baithe rehna aur bilkul physical movement na hona.
- Subah ki toilet urge ko roka jaana ya time hi na dena.
- Irregular meal timing aur raat ko bahut late khana.
- Stress, anxiety aur neend puri na hona.
- Bahut sookha, thanda ya baasi khana.

## Changes To Make
- Do: din bhar 8–10 glass halka garam paani.
- Do: subah ek fix time par toilet jaayein, chahe urge na ho.
- Do: har meal mein sabzi, dal aur fibre wali cheezein.
- Do: roz 30 minute walk ya koi movement.
- Avoid: toilet ki urge ko rokna.
- Avoid: maida, biscuit, fast food aur bahut zyada chai.
- Avoid: raat ko bahut late aur bhari khana.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** papita, kela (paka hua), anjeer aur munakka (bhige hue), amrud, palak
aur hari sabziyan, daliya, oats, moong dal, ghee ke saath garam khana, alsi
(flax) powder, garam paani.

**Avoid:** maida, white bread, biscuit, bahut zyada chawal, tala hua khana,
packet aur processed food, zyada chai/coffee, kacche kele, thanda aur sookha
khana.

## Daily Routine
- Subah uthte hi: 1–2 glass halka garam paani, bina kuch khaye.
- 10–15 minute baad: toilet jaane ki koshish, bina jaldbaazi ke.
- Subah: 20 minute yoga ya walk.
- Dopahar: bada meal 12–2 ke beech, sabzi aur dal ke saath.
- Shaam: bhige hue munakka ya anjeer.
- Raat: dinner 8 baje se pehle, halka aur garam. Ek chammach ghee garam doodh
  ke saath sone se pehle.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Malasana (squat), Pawanmuktasana, Bhujangasana, Dhanurasana,
  Ardha Matsyendrasana, Balasana, Vajrasana.
- Pranayama: deep belly breathing, Anulom Vilom, khali pet halka Kapalbhati.
- Roz subah 20 minute — movement hi vata ko neeche ki taraf chalata hai.
- Avoid: pregnancy ya piles mein zor wale twists — pehle doctor se poochein.

## Home Remedies
- Raat bhar bhige 5–6 munakka ya 2 anjeer, subah khali pet.
- Sone se pehle garam doodh mein 1 chammach ghee.
- Subah garam paani mein nimbu.
- Khane mein ghee aur pakayi hui sabzi ki matra badhana.
- Alsi (flax seed) powder khane ke saath.
- Koi laxative churna, tablet ya brand khud se na lein — aadat pad jati hai.

## When To See A Doctor
- Stool mein khoon, ya kaala/tar jaisa stool.
- Bina wajah weight kam hona.
- Kabz jo achanak shuru ho aur pehle kabhi na hui ho, khaas kar 45 ki umar ke baad.
- Tez pet dard, ulti, aur gas/stool bilkul band — turant hospital jaayein.
- Kabz aur diarrhoea baari-baari se chalta rahe.
- 2 hafte se zyada kabz jo in changes se bhi na sudhre.
