# Stress & Anxiety (Chinta / Manasika Vyadhi)

## What It Is
- Anxiety mein vata dosha mann aur nervous system mein badh jata hai — sochne
  ki raftaar tez ho jaati hai aur body alert mode mein atak jaati hai.
- Lakshan: dil tez dhadakna, haath-pair thande, saans chhoti, neend na aana,
  pet kharab, aur mann mein baar-baar wahi soch.
- Ayurveda ka nazariya: irregular routine aur vata badhane wali aadatein mann ko
  bhi asthir kar deti hain — isliye ilaaj routine se shuru hota hai.

## Root Cause
- Roz ka routine bilkul unfix hona — sona, uthna, khana sab badalta rehna.
- Neend puri na hona.
- Bahut zyada screen time, khaas kar raat ko aur news/social media.
- Zyada chai, coffee aur energy drinks.
- Khana skip karna — blood sugar girne se ghabrahat badhti hai.
- Kaam ya padhai ka lagatar pressure, bina break ke.
- Akela mehsoos karna, baat na kar paana.
- Bilkul physical activity na hona.

## Changes To Make
- Do: sone-uthne ka time fix karein — vata ke liye yeh sabse badi dawa hai.
- Do: roz 20–30 minute walk ya koi movement.
- Do: din mein 3 baar 2 minute ke liye ruk kar 10 lambi saansein.
- Do: kisi bharose ke insaan se baat karein — akele mat rakhein.
- Do: sone se 1 ghanta pehle phone door.
- Avoid: raat ko news aur social media scroll karna.
- Avoid: shaam ke baad chai/coffee.
- Avoid: khana skip karna.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** garam pakaya hua taza khana, ghee, doodh (halka garam, haldi ya
jaiphal ke saath), bhige badam aur akhrot, khajoor, kela, oats, khichdi,
moong dal, meethe fal, nariyal paani, tulsi aur brahmi ki chai.

**Avoid:** zyada chai/coffee aur energy drinks, alcohol, cigarette, packet aur
processed food, bahut zyada meetha aur maida, thanda-sookha khana, raat ko
bhari ya bahut late khana.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: fix time par uthein, phone turant na dekhein.
- Subah: 10 minute dhoop mein baithna ya walk.
- Subah: 20 minute yoga aur pranayama.
- Nashta time par — khali pet ghabrahat badhati hai.
- Dopahar: bada meal 12–2 ke beech, shaanti se baith kar.
- Shaam: walk ya koi hobby, screen se hat kar.
- Raat: halka garam dinner 8 baje tak.
- Sone se pehle: pairon aur sar ki til ke tel se maalish, garam doodh, 10:30 tak neend.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Balasana, Shavasana, Viparita Karani, Setu Bandhasana, Paschimottanasana,
  Sukhasana mein shaant baithna, Marjariasana.
- Pranayama: Bhramari (sabse asardar anxiety ke liye), Anulom Vilom, slow deep
  belly breathing, Chandra Bhedana.
- Ghabrahat ke waqt turant: 4 count andar saans, 6 count bahar — 10 baar.
- Roz 10 minute Yoga Nidra ya simple meditation.
- Avoid: bahut tez Bhastrika aur over-stimulating practice jab pehle se ghabrahat ho.

## Home Remedies
- Sone se pehle garam doodh mein chutki haldi ya jaiphal.
- Til ya nariyal tel se sar aur pairon ki maalish, raat ko.
- Tulsi ki chai ya brahmi ka paani.
- Roz 5–6 bhige badam.
- Din mein 10 minute dhoop.
- Kaagaz par likh dena jo mann mein chal raha hai — mann halka hota hai.
- Koi anti-anxiety tablet, syrup ya brand khud se na lein.

## When To See A Doctor
- Khud ko ya kisi aur ko nuksaan pahunchane ke vichar — turant help lein.
- Panic attacks jo baar-baar ho rahe hon.
- Anxiety itni ki roz ka kaam, job ya padhai ruk jaaye.
- Neend hafton se bilkul na aa rahi ho.
- Chest pain, saans phoolna ya bahut tez dil dhadakna — pehle heart check
  karwana zaroori hai, isko sirf anxiety maan kar mat chhodein.
- Nashe ya alcohol ka sahara lena pad raha ho.
- Yeh lifestyle steps madad karte hain, lekin proper counselling ya doctor ke
  ilaaj ki jagah nahi lete.
