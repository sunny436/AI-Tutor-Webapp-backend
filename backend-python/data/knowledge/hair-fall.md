# Hair Fall (Khalitya)

## What It Is
- Din mein 50–100 baal girna bilkul normal hai. Isse zyada, ya sar par khaali
  jagah dikhna, hair fall maana jata hai.
- Ayurveda mein baal "asthi dhatu" (haddi) ka upvridhi hain, aur baal jhadne ki
  jad aksar badha hua pitta aur kamzor poshan hoti hai.
- Sach ye hai: hair fall ka ilaaj bahar se lagane se nahi, andar se — khana,
  neend, stress aur digestion theek karne se hota hai.

## Root Cause
- Protein aur iron ki kami — sabse aam wajah, khaas kar ladies mein.
- Khoon ki kami (anaemia) aur vitamin D/B12 ki kami.
- Thyroid ki problem.
- Lagatar stress aur neend ki kami.
- Ladies mein PCOS aur hormone imbalance.
- Delivery ke baad ka samay (yeh 6–9 mahine mein apne aap theek hota hai).
- Bimari, bukhar ya bade weight loss ke 2–3 mahine baad.
- Bahut zyada chemical treatment: colour, straightening, smoothening.
- Bahut tight choti/ponytail, aur roz garam tools.
- Gande ya bahut hard paani se baal dhona.
- Family history (male/female pattern baldness).
- Sar mein dandruff aur fungal infection.

## Changes To Make
- Do: har meal mein protein — dal, anda, paneer, sprouts.
- Do: hafte mein 2 baar tel se sar ki halki maalish.
- Do: baal dhone ke liye halka shampoo aur normal paani (bahut garam nahi).
- Do: 7–8 ghante neend aur roz stress kam karne ki practice.
- Do: baal geele hon to halke se sukhayein, zor se na ragdein.
- Avoid: bahut tight choti, bun ya ponytail.
- Avoid: baar-baar colour, straightening aur chemical treatment.
- Avoid: roz hair dryer aur straightener ki garmi.
- Avoid: crash diet — baal sabse pehle iska shikaar hote hain.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** moong aur masoor dal, rajma-chana, anda, paneer, dahi, sprouts,
palak aur hari sabziyan, chukandar, amla (roz), badam-akhrot, alsi aur kaddu
ke beej, til, khajoor aur kishmish, anaar, nariyal, ragi, ghee, curry patta,
methi, khoob paani.

**Avoid:** crash diet aur khana skip karna, packet aur processed food, zyada
cheeni aur maida, bahut zyada chai/coffee, cold drinks, bahut teekha aur tala
hua (pitta badhata hai), alcohol aur cigarette.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: bhige badam, amla ya amla juice khali pet.
- Subah: 20–30 minute yoga ya walk — sar tak blood flow badhta hai.
- Nashta: protein wala — anda, moong chilla, sprouts ya paneer.
- Dopahar: dal, sabzi, dahi aur salad.
- Shaam: nuts aur seeds ka chhota snack.
- Raat: halka dinner 8 baje tak.
- Raat: 5 minute ungliyon se sar ki halki maalish (bina tel bhi chalega).
- Hafte mein 2 baar: raat ko tel lagayein, subah dho lein.
- 10:30 tak neend.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Adho Mukha Svanasana (downward dog), Uttanasana (aage jhukna),
  Balasana, Sarvangasana, Viparita Karani, Setu Bandhasana, Vajrasana,
  Bhujangasana.
- Sar neeche jaane wale poses sar tak khoon pahunchate hain — yahi asli fayda hai.
- Pranayama: Anulom Vilom, Bhramari, Kapalbhati (khali pet), Shitali (pitta ke liye).
- Balayam (dono haathon ke naakhun aapas mein ragadna) roz 5 minute.
- Roz 10 minute meditation — stress hair fall ka bada karan hai.
- High BP, gardan ki chot ya aankh ki problem ho to inversions se pehle poochein.

## Home Remedies
- Nariyal ya til tel halka garam karke sar ki ungliyon se 5–10 minute maalish,
  hafte mein 2 baar.
- Tel mein curry patta ya methi daal kar garam karke thanda hone par lagana.
- Amla — roz taza, juice ya powder. Ayurveda mein baalon ke liye sabse khaas.
- Methi ke daane raat bhar bhigo kar peeskar sar par 30 minute, phir dho lein.
- Pyaaz ka ras 20–30 minute, phir dho lein (kuch logon ko suit nahi karta —
  pehle thoda sa test karein).
- Aloe vera gel sar par.
- Roz 5–6 bhige badam aur ek amla.
- Koi hair growth tablet, serum brand ya dosage khud se na lein.

## When To See A Doctor
- Sar par gol khaali patch ban jaana (alopecia areata).
- Bahut tezi se baal jhadna, ya mutthi bhar ke hisaab se girna.
- Baal ke saath sar mein khujli, papdi, mawad ya dard (infection).
- Baal jhadne ke saath bahut thakan, thand lagna aur weight badhna — thyroid test.
- Ladies mein baal jhadne ke saath irregular period aur chehre par baal — PCOS test.
- Bahut peela dikhna, chakkar aur saans phoolna — khoon ki kami ka test.
- Bhaunh aur palkon ke baal bhi jhadna.
- Test zaroor karwayein: thyroid, khoon ki kami (Hb, ferritin), vitamin D aur
  B12. In chaaron ki kami hair fall ki sabse aam wajah hai aur inka proper
  ilaaj hota hai.
