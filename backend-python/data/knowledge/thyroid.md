# Thyroid Problems (Galaganda / Thyroid Vikara)

## What It Is
- Thyroid gale mein titli jaisi ek chhoti gland hai jo body ki poori speed
  (metabolism) control karti hai.
- **Hypothyroid (kam kaam karna):** thakan, weight badhna, thand lagna, kabz,
  baal jhadna, sookhi skin, udasi, ladies mein bhaari period.
- **Hyperthyroid (zyada kaam karna):** weight girna, dil tez dhadakna, haath
  kaapna, garmi lagna, ghabrahat, neend na aana, loose motions.
- Ayurveda mein isko kapha-vata ka bigadna aur kamzor agni maana jata hai.
- **Yeh page lifestyle support ke liye hai.** Thyroid ka diagnosis blood test se
  hota hai aur ilaaj sirf doctor deta hai. Thyroid ki dawa kabhi apne aap band
  ya kam na karein.

## Root Cause
- Autoimmune wajah (Hashimoto ya Graves) — sabse aam, jisme body apni hi gland
  par hamla karti hai.
- Iodine ki kami ya bahut zyada iodine.
- Lagatar stress aur neend ki kami.
- Delivery ke baad ka samay.
- Family history.
- Selenium, zinc, iron aur vitamin D ki kami.
- Kuch dawaiyan aur gale ki radiation.

## Changes To Make
- Do: dawa roz ek hi time par, khali pet, aur uske 45–60 minute tak kuch na khaayein.
- Do: thyroid ki dawa aur calcium/iron ke beech kam se kam 4 ghante ka gap.
- Do: roz 30–45 minute walk ya yoga.
- Do: 7–8 ghante neend aur stress kam karne ki practice.
- Do: doctor ke bataye time par TSH test karwate rahein.
- Avoid: apne aap dawa ki dose badalna ya band karna.
- Avoid: bina test ke iodine ya seaweed supplement lena.
- Avoid: kachhi gobhi, patta gobhi, broccoli aur soya bahut zyada matra mein —
  pakane se yeh problem kaafi kam ho jaati hai.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** iodine wala namak, moong dal, hari sabziyan (pakayi hui), brazil nut
aur kaddu ke beej (selenium-zinc ke liye), badam-akhrot, nariyal aur nariyal tel,
amla, dhaniya, jeera, haldi, ghee, dahi, anda, machhli, ragi, jau, taza fal,
khoob paani.

**Avoid:** kachhi gobhi/patta gobhi/broccoli/mooli bahut zyada, zyada soya aur
soya milk, packet aur processed food, zyada cheeni aur maida, alcohol aur
cigarette, bahut zyada chai/coffee (khaas kar hyperthyroid mein), gluten agar
autoimmune thyroid ho aur doctor salah de.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: uthte hi thyroid ki dawa khali pet, paani ke saath.
- 45–60 minute baad: nashta.
- Subah: 30–45 minute walk ya yoga.
- Dopahar 12–2: bada meal — dal, sabzi, roti, salad.
- Shaam: nuts ya fal ka chhota snack.
- Raat 8 baje tak: halka dinner.
- Raat: pairon ki tel maalish, screen band, 10:30 tak neend.
- Roz 10–15 minute dhoop.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas (hypothyroid ke liye — gale par halka dabav): Sarvangasana,
  Halasana, Matsyasana, Setu Bandhasana, Ustrasana, Bhujangasana,
  Viparita Karani, Marjariasana.
- Asanas (hyperthyroid ke liye — shaant karne wale): Balasana, Shavasana,
  Supta Baddha Konasana, Viparita Karani, Setu Bandhasana. Gale par dabav wale
  poses se bachein.
- Pranayama: Ujjayi (gale par seedha kaam karta hai), Anulom Vilom, Bhramari.
  Hypothyroid mein Kapalbhati bhi. Hyperthyroid mein Shitali aur Sheetkari.
- Roz 10 minute meditation.
- Avoid: gardan ki chot, high BP, aankh ki problem ya pregnancy mein Sarvangasana
  aur Halasana — pehle doctor ya teacher se poochein.

## Home Remedies
- Dhaniya ke beej raat bhar bhigo kar uska paani subah.
- Nariyal tel khane mein — metabolism ke liye madadgar.
- Amla juice ya taza amla roz.
- 2 brazil nut ya kaddu ke beej roz (selenium ke liye).
- Ujjayi pranayama roz 10 minute.
- Haldi wala garam doodh.
- Koi thyroid supplement, ashwagandha ya kelp brand khud se na lein — hyperthyroid
  mein kuch cheezein nuksaan karti hain. Pehle doctor se poochein.

## When To See A Doctor
- Gale mein gaanth, sujan, ya awaaz bhaari ho jaana.
- Nigalne ya saans lene mein dikkat.
- Dil bahut tez dhadakna, chest pain, ya saans phoolna — turant.
- Bahut zyada thakan jo aaram se bhi na jaaye.
- Weight ka tezi se badhna ya girna.
- Ladies mein period bilkul band ho jaana ya bahut bhaari bleeding.
- Pregnancy plan kar rahi hon ya pregnant hon — thyroid ki dose badalni padti
  hai, turant doctor ko batayein.
- Aankhein bahar nikalna ya dhundhla dikhna (Graves ka sign).
- Thyroid ki jaanch sirf blood test (TSH, T3, T4) se hoti hai — lakshan dekh kar
  andaza mat lagayein, test karwayein.
