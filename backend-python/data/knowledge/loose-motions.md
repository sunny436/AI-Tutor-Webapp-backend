# Loose Motions & Diarrhoea (Atisar)

## What It Is
- Loose motions mein digestion ki aag (agni) kamzor pad jati hai aur khana theek
  se pacha nahi jaata.
- Body us bina pacha khana ko paani ke saath jaldi bahar nikaal deti hai.
- Ayurveda isko "atisar" kehta hai. Sabse badi baat: body ka paani aur namak
  bahar ja raha hai, isliye rehydration sabse pehle.

## Root Cause
- Baasi, bahar ka ya galat tarike se rakha hua khana.
- Ganda ya untreated paani.
- Bahut zyada teekha, tala hua ya bhari khana ek saath.
- Milk products jab digestion pehle se kamzor ho.
- Stress aur anxiety — pet seedha react karta hai.
- Mausam badalna ya achanak khane ki aadat badalna.

## Changes To Make
- Do: sabse pehle paani aur namak wapas bharein — thoda thoda har 10–15 minute.
- Do: 1–2 din tak sirf halka, garam, simple khana.
- Do: sab kuch uble hue ya filter kiye paani se.
- Do: aaram karein, body ko energy bachane dein.
- Avoid: doodh, cheese aur bhari dairy jab tak stool normal na ho.
- Avoid: tala hua, teekha, packet food aur bahar ka khana.
- Avoid: cold drink, coffee aur juice — yeh aur paani kheenchte hain.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** patli khichdi, chawal ka paani (maand), moong dal ka paani, chaas
(halka namak aur bhuna jeera), paka kela, uble hue chawal, anaar, saada toast,
nariyal paani, ORS jaisa ghar ka namak-cheeni paani.

**Avoid:** doodh, paneer, cheese, tala hua khana, teekha aur masaledar khana,
raw salad, cold drinks, coffee, fruit juice, packet snacks, non-veg jab tak
theek na ho.

## Daily Routine
- Har 10–15 minute: thoda paani, nariyal paani ya namak-cheeni ka ghol.
- Subah aur dopahar: patli khichdi ya chawal ka paani.
- Din bhar: aaram, koi bhaari kaam ya exercise nahi.
- Shaam: chaas mein bhuna jeera aur chutki namak.
- Raat: bahut halka dinner, jaldi.
- Stool normal hone ke 2–3 din baad hi normal khana wapas shuru karein, dheere se.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Acute phase mein yoga nahi — sirf aaram. Body ko force mat karein.
- Theek hone ke baad: Vajrasana, Balasana, Supta Baddha Konasana.
- Pranayama: slow deep belly breathing, Anulom Vilom — yeh gut ko shaant karta hai.
- Avoid: Kapalbhati, Bhastrika, strong twists aur inversions jab tak poori tarah
  theek na ho jaayein.

## Home Remedies
- Chaas mein bhuna jeera powder aur chutki sendha namak — din mein 2–3 baar.
- Chawal ka maand (rice water) thode namak ke saath.
- Paka kela — potassium wapas karta hai.
- Anaar ka daana ya juice.
- Ghar ka ORS: 1 litre uble paani + 6 chammach cheeni + aadha chammach namak.
- Koi anti-diarrhoea tablet ya syrup khud se na lein.

## When To See A Doctor
- Stool mein khoon ya bilkul kaala stool.
- Tez bukhar ke saath loose motions.
- Dehydration ke signs: mooh bilkul sookha, urine bahut kam ya bilkul nahi,
  chakkar, ya bahut kamzori — turant hospital.
- Chhote bachche, buzurg ya pregnancy mein loose motions — turant doctor.
- 2 din se zyada chale, ya baar-baar ulti ke saath ho.
- Tez lagatar pet dard.
