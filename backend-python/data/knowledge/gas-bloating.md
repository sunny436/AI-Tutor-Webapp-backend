# Gas & Bloating (Adhmana)

## What It Is
- Gas aur bloating mein vata dosha pet mein ruk jata hai aur hawa bahar nikalne
  ki jagah andar ghoomti rehti hai.
- Pet bhara-bhara, tight aur phoola hua lagta hai, kabhi kabhi halka dard bhi.
- Ayurveda ke hisaab se yeh kamzor "agni" (digestive fire) ka seedha nateeja hai.

## Root Cause
- Jaldi-jaldi khana aur theek se na chabana — hawa khane ke saath andar jaati hai.
- Thanda, sookha, raw ya baasi khana jo vata badhata hai.
- Bina bhige ya kam pake rajma, chole, chana aur other beans.
- Zyada cold drinks, soda aur khane ke saath bahut paani.
- Irregular meal timing aur raat ko late bhari khana.
- Stress aur neend ki kami — vata aur bigadta hai.
- Din bhar baithe rehna, bilkul walk na karna.

## Changes To Make
- Do: aaram se baith kar khana, har niwala achhe se chabana.
- Do: khane ke baad 10 minute slow walk.
- Do: beans/rajma ko raat bhar bhigo kar, hing aur ajwain daal kar pakana.
- Do: garam pakaya hua khana khana.
- Avoid: khane ke saath zyada paani ya cold drink.
- Avoid: raat ko salad, sprouts aur thanda khana.
- Avoid: khana kha kar turant baith jaana ya let jaana.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** moong dal, khichdi, garam pakayi sabziyan (lauki, tori, kaddu), chaas,
adrak, ajwain, jeera, hing, saunf, dhaniya, kali mirch, garam soup.

**Avoid:** rajma, chole, chana aur zyada beans, gobhi/patta gobhi/broccoli,
cold drinks aur soda, raat ko raw salad, maida, packet ka processed food,
zyada dairy aur cheese.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: garam paani mein thoda ajwain ya jeera daal kar peena.
- Subah: 15–20 minute yoga, khaas kar pet wale asanas.
- Dopahar: sabse bada meal 12–2 ke beech, garam aur taza.
- Har meal ke baad: 5–10 minute Vajrasana ya dheemi walk.
- Raat: halka dinner 7–8 baje tak, khichdi ya soup jaisa.
- Raat: sone se pehle pet par halke haath se ghadi ki direction mein maalish.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Pawanmuktasana (gas nikalne wala pose), Vajrasana, Apanasana,
  Balasana, Supta Matsyendrasana (halka twist), Malasana.
- Pranayama: deep belly breathing, Anulom Vilom, halka Kapalbhati (khali pet).
- Avoid: khane ke turant baad koi bhi strong twist ya inversion.

## Home Remedies
- Ajwain aur kaala namak garam paani ke saath.
- Khane ke baad saunf ya jeera chabana.
- Adrak ka chhota tukda sendha namak ke saath khane se pehle.
- Hing ki chutki garam paani mein — pet ke bharipan ke liye.
- Jeera-dhaniya-saunf ka paani din bhar sip karna.
- Koi churna brand, tablet ya dosage khud se na lein.

## When To See A Doctor
- Tez ya lagatar pet dard jo kam na ho.
- Stool mein khoon, kaala stool, ya ulti mein khoon.
- Bina wajah weight kam hona.
- Pet ekdum sakht ho jaana aur gas/latrine bilkul band ho jaana — turant hospital.
- Bukhar ke saath pet dard, ya lagatar ulti.
- Bloating jo hafton se ho aur in changes se bhi na sudhre.
