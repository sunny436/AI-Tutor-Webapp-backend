# Indigestion & Weak Digestion (Ajirna / Mandagni)

## What It Is
- Ayurveda mein digestion ki taakat ko "agni" (digestive fire) kehte hain.
- Jab agni kamzor pad jaati hai to khana theek se nahi pachta aur andar
  "ama" (bina pacha chipchipa waste) jama hota hai.
- Signs: khane ke baad bhaari lagna, bhookh na lagna, jeebh par safed parat,
  susti, aur mooh ka swaad kharab.

## Root Cause
- Pichla khana pachne se pehle hi doosra khana kha lena.
- Bhookh na hone par bhi khana, ya zaroorat se zyada khana.
- Thanda, baasi, bacha hua aur packet ka khana.
- Khane ke saath thanda paani ya cold drink — agni bujha deta hai.
- Raat ko bahut late aur bhari dinner.
- Tension, jaldbaazi aur TV/phone dekhte hue khana.
- Din bhar bilkul movement na hona.

## Changes To Make
- Do: sirf tab khaayein jab sach mein bhookh lagi ho.
- Do: pet ka 3/4 hissa hi bharein, thoda khali chhodein.
- Do: garam, taza pakaya hua khana khaayein.
- Do: har niwala achhe se chabayein, khaate waqt phone side mein rakhein.
- Do: do meals ke beech 4–5 ghante ka gap.
- Avoid: khane ke saath thanda paani; halka garam paani lein.
- Avoid: bina bhookh ke snacking aur raat ko bhari khana.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** khichdi, moong dal, garam pakayi sabzi, daliya, adrak, jeera, ajwain,
kali mirch, hing, saunf, haldi, chaas, halka garam paani, thoda ghee.

**Avoid:** thanda aur baasi khana, fridge se seedha nikala khana, cold drinks,
ice cream, cheese aur bhari dairy, maida, packet snacks, bahut zyada meetha,
raat ko non-veg aur heavy khana.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: halka garam paani, phir 20 minute yoga ya walk.
- Khane se pehle: adrak ka chhota tukda sendha namak ke saath — agni jagata hai.
- Dopahar: sabse bada meal 12–2 ke beech (agni sabse strong hota hai).
- Har meal ke baad: 5–10 minute Vajrasana, phir dheemi walk.
- Shaam: halka snack, bhari nahi.
- Raat: sabse halka meal 7–8 baje tak; 10:30 tak so jaayein.
- Hafte mein ek din sirf khichdi ya fruits — agni ko reset milta hai.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Vajrasana (khane ke baad), Pawanmuktasana, Bhujangasana, Dhanurasana,
  Ardha Matsyendrasana, Ushtrasana.
- Pranayama: khali pet Kapalbhati (dheere se shuru), Bhastrika, Anulom Vilom,
  Agnisar kriya.
- Roz subah khali pet 20 minute — yeh seedha agni par kaam karta hai.
- Avoid: khane ke turant baad koi bhi strong asana.

## Home Remedies
- Khane se 15 minute pehle: adrak + sendha namak + nimbu ki chhoti si slice.
- Jeera, dhaniya aur saunf ka paani din bhar sip karna.
- Khane ke baad saunf ya ajwain chabana.
- Halka garam paani din bhar, thanda paani band.
- Haldi wala garam doodh raat ko.
- Koi digestive churna brand, tablet ya dosage khud se na lein.

## When To See A Doctor
- Bina wajah weight kam hona ya bhookh bilkul khatam ho jaana.
- Ulti mein khoon, kaala stool, ya nigalne mein dikkat.
- Lagatar pet dard jo hafton se ho.
- Peeli aankhein ya peeli skin (jaundice ke signs).
- Khane ke baad har baar ulti hona.
- Digestion ki problem jo 3–4 hafte in changes ke baad bhi na sudhre.
