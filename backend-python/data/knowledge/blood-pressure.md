# High Blood Pressure (Raktachapa Vriddhi)

## What It Is
- BP mein khoon nason par zaroorat se zyada dabav dalta hai. 140/90 se upar
  lagatar rehna high BP maana jata hai.
- Isko "silent killer" kehte hain kyunki aksar koi lakshan nahi hote, par yeh
  chupchap heart, kidney, dimaag aur aankhon ko nuksaan pahunchata rehta hai.
- Ayurveda mein isko vata aur pitta ka badhna maana jata hai, jise stress,
  namak aur bigda hua routine badhate hain.
- **Yeh page lifestyle support ke liye hai.** BP ki dawa kabhi apne aap band ya
  kam na karein — isse stroke ya heart attack ho sakta hai.

## Root Cause
- Zyada namak, khaas kar packet food, achaar, papad aur namkeen se.
- Weight badhna aur exercise bilkul na hona.
- Lagatar stress, gussa aur neend ki kami.
- Alcohol aur cigarette.
- Zyada tala hua, packet aur processed khana.
- Family history.
- Diabetes, kidney ki problem, ya thyroid.
- Kuch painkiller aur dawaiyan bhi BP badhati hain.

## Changes To Make
- Do: namak kam karein — din mein 1 chhoti chammach (5 gram) se zyada nahi.
- Do: roz 30–45 minute brisk walk. BP par iska seedha asar hota hai.
- Do: BP ghar par regular check karein aur ek diary mein likhein.
- Do: 7–8 ghante neend aur roz 10 minute shaant baithna.
- Do: weight kam karein agar zyada hai — 5 kg kam hone se bhi BP girta hai.
- Do: dawa roz ek hi time par, bina naaga.
- Avoid: table par upar se namak dalna, achaar, papad, chips, namkeen.
- Avoid: packet soup, sauce, ready-to-eat aur processed food (chhupa hua namak).
- Avoid: alcohol aur cigarette.
- Avoid: bina doctor se poochhe painkiller lena.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** kela, nariyal paani, palak aur hari sabziyan, chukandar, lauki, kheera,
lehsun, oats aur jau, moong dal, rajma-chana, badam-akhrot, alsi aur kaddu ke
beej, dahi aur chaas (bina namak), amla, anaar, seb, tarbooz, haldi, dhaniya,
khoob saada paani.

**Avoid:** namak zyada, achaar, papad, chips, namkeen, packet soup aur sauce,
processed aur ready-to-eat food, bakery aur cake, red meat aur organ meat,
deep-fried khana, alcohol, zyada chai/coffee aur energy drinks, cold drinks.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: fix time par uthein, halka garam paani.
- Subah: 30–45 minute brisk walk (bahut tez running nahi).
- Subah: 20 minute yoga aur pranayama.
- Nashta: oats, daliya ya chilla — bina zyada namak.
- Dopahar 12–2: bada meal, salad ke saath.
- Shaam: nuts ya fal, bina namak.
- Raat 7–8 baje: halka dinner.
- Raat: pairon ki tel maalish, screen band, 10:30 tak neend.
- Roz ek fix time par BP check karein aur note karein.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Shavasana (roz 10 minute — BP ke liye sabse achha), Balasana,
  Setu Bandhasana, Viparita Karani, Supta Baddha Konasana, Marjariasana,
  Sukhasana mein shaant baithna, Paschimottanasana (halka), Tadasana.
- BP ke liye shaant aur dheemi practice chahiye, tez nahi.
- Pranayama: Anulom Vilom (sabse madadgar), Bhramari, Shitali, slow deep
  belly breathing — 4 count andar, 6 count bahar.
- Roz 10–15 minute meditation ya Yoga Nidra.
- **Avoid:** Sirsasana, Sarvangasana aur koi bhi inversion. Kapalbhati aur
  Bhastrika tez nahi karein. Saans rokna (kumbhaka) bilkul nahi. Zor lagane
  wale aur ulta latakne wale poses BP mein khatarnak hain.

## Home Remedies
- Lehsun ki 1–2 kali subah khali pet paani ke saath.
- Nariyal paani roz.
- Amla juice ya taza amla.
- Chukandar ka juice.
- Roz 10 minute Anulom Vilom aur Shavasana.
- Alsi powder 1 chammach roz.
- Namak ki jagah nimbu, kali mirch aur herbs se swaad badhayein.
- Koi BP ka supplement ya brand khud se na lein, aur doctor ki dawa band na karein.

## When To See A Doctor
- BP 180/120 se upar — turant hospital jaayein, chahe koi lakshan na ho.
- Tez sar dard ke saath dhundhla dikhna ya ulti.
- Chest mein dard ya dabav, khaas kar haath/jabde tak jaane wala — emergency.
- Saans phoolna, khaas kar letne par.
- Achanak ek taraf kamzori, chehra tedha hona, bolne mein dikkat — stroke ka
  sign, turant hospital, ek minute bhi na gawayein.
- Naak se baar-baar khoon aana.
- Pairon ya chehre par sujan, ya urine bahut kam hona (kidney ka sign).
- Pregnancy mein BP badhna — turant doctor, yeh maa aur bachche dono ke liye
  serious hai.
- BP ki dawa se koi side effect lag raha ho to dawa band na karein — doctor se
  milein aur wahi badlaav karenge.
