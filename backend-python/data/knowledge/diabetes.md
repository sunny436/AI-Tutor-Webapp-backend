# Diabetes / High Blood Sugar (Madhumeha)

## What It Is
- Diabetes mein khoon mein sugar (glucose) ka level lagatar zyada rehta hai,
  kyunki insulin ya to kam banta hai ya theek se kaam nahi karta.
- Ayurveda mein isko "madhumeha" kehte hain — kapha aur meda (fat) ka badhna
  aur dheemi agni iski jad hai.
- **Yeh page sirf lifestyle support ke liye hai.** Diabetes ka diagnosis aur
  ilaaj sirf doctor karta hai. Doctor ki di hui dawa kabhi apne aap band ya kam
  na karein — yeh jaanleva ho sakta hai.

## Root Cause
- Din bhar baithe rehna aur bilkul exercise na hona.
- Cheeni, maida, cold drinks, packet juice aur bakery ka zyada istemaal.
- Weight badhna, khaas kar pet ke aas-paas fat.
- Neend puri na hona aur lagatar stress.
- Family history — parents ko diabetes ho to risk zyada.
- Ladies mein PCOS ya pregnancy ke time ka diabetes.
- Baar-baar khana aur raat ko late bhaari dinner.

## Changes To Make
- Do: roz 45 minute walk ya exercise — sugar control mein yeh dawa jitna hi zaroori hai.
- Do: khane ke baad 15 minute walk — sugar spike seedha kam hota hai.
- Do: har meal mein fibre aur protein pehle, carbs baad mein.
- Do: 5–10% weight kam karein agar zyada hai.
- Do: sugar regular check karein aur record rakhein.
- Do: doctor ki dawa aur check-up bilkul time par.
- Do: 7–8 ghante neend.
- Avoid: cheeni, mithai, cold drinks, packet juice, maida aur bakery.
- Avoid: fruit juice — poora fal khaayein, juice nahi.
- Avoid: nange pair chalna aur pairon ki chot ko ignore karna.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** jau (barley), bajra, ragi, oats, chana ka aata, moong dal, hari
sabziyan, methi ke daane, karela, lauki, jamun (aur jamun ki guthli), amla,
dalchini, haldi, alsi aur kaddu ke beej, badam-akhrot, paneer, anda, chaas,
kam meethe fal (jamun, amrud, papita) thodi matra mein.

**Avoid:** cheeni, gud aur shahad, mithai, cold drinks aur packet juice, maida,
white bread, biscuit-cake, white chawal zyada, aloo aur shakarkandi zyada,
kela-aam-cheeku-angoor zyada, fried aur fast food, alcohol.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: methi ka bhiga paani ya halka garam paani.
- Subah: 45 minute brisk walk ya exercise.
- Nashta 8–9 baje: protein aur fibre wala (moong chilla, besan chilla, oats).
- Dopahar 12–2: bada meal — salad pehle, phir dal, sabzi, jau/bajra ki roti.
- Har meal ke baad: 15 minute walk.
- Shaam 5 baje: nuts ya chaas ka chhota snack.
- Raat 7–8 baje: halka dinner, sabzi aur dal zyada, roti kam.
- Raat: 10:30 tak neend.
- Roz: pairon ko dekhein — koi cut, chhala ya kaala nishan to nahi.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Surya Namaskar (roz 12 round), Mandukasana (pancreas ke liye khaas),
  Vakrasana, Ardha Matsyendrasana, Dhanurasana, Bhujangasana, Paschimottanasana,
  Halasana, Setu Bandhasana.
- Pet aur pancreas par dabav wale twists sabse zyada madad karte hain.
- Pranayama: Kapalbhati (khali pet), Bhastrika, Anulom Vilom, Bhramari.
- Roz kam se kam 30–45 minute, regular.
- Avoid: agar aankh (retinopathy) ki problem ho to Sirsasana aur inversions —
  pehle doctor se poochein. Pairon mein sunnpan ho to balance wale poses dhyan se.

## Home Remedies
- Methi ke daane raat bhar bhigo kar subah khali pet paani ke saath.
- Karela ka juice ya sabzi (kam matra mein, roz nahi).
- Jamun ki guthli ka powder — mausam mein taza jamun.
- Dalchini ka paani.
- Amla juice ya taza amla roz.
- Khane ke baad walk — sabse asardar "remedy".
- **Bahut zaroori:** yeh sab dawa ke saath chalte hain, uski jagah nahi. Agar
  yeh cheezein shuru karein to sugar aur bhi zyada baar check karein, kyunki
  sugar bahut neeche bhi ja sakta hai. Doctor ko batayein.

## When To See A Doctor
- Sugar bahut zyada (300+) ya bahut kam (70 se neeche) aana.
- Low sugar ke signs: paseena, kaanpna, chakkar, confusion — turant kuch meetha
  khaayein aur doctor se milein.
- Bahut pyaas, baar-baar urine, bahut thakan aur weight girna.
- Dhundhla dikhna ya aankh mein achanak badlav.
- Pairon mein sunnpan, jhunjhuni, ya koi zakhm jo bhar na raha ho — diabetes
  mein yeh serious hai, turant dikhayein.
- Chest pain, saans phoolna ya pairon mein sujan.
- Bukhar ya koi infection — diabetes mein infection tezi se badhta hai.
- Har 3 mahine HbA1c, aur saal mein ek baar aankh, kidney aur pair ka check-up
  zaroor karwayein.
