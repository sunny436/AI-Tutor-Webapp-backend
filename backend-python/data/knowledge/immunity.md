# Immunity & Frequent Illness (Vyadhikshamatva / Ojas)

## What It Is
- Immunity matlab body ki wo taakat jo bimari ko rokti hai, aur bimar hone par
  jaldi theek karti hai.
- Ayurveda mein isko "ojas" kehte hain — achhe digestion se bana sabse sookshma
  saar. Kamzor agni = kamzor ojas = baar-baar bimari.
- Isliye Ayurveda mein immunity ka rasta pet se hokar jata hai, kisi tonic se nahi.
- Sach: koi bhi cheez "instant immunity" nahi deti. Yeh roz ki aadaton se
  dheere-dheere banti hai.

## Root Cause
- Kamzor digestion aur kabz — ojas ban hi nahi paata.
- Neend puri na hona — immunity par sabse seedha asar.
- Lagatar stress.
- Poshan ki kami: protein, iron, zinc, vitamin C aur D.
- Vitamin D ki kami — India mein bahut aam.
- Bilkul exercise na hona, ya bahut zyada intense exercise.
- Packet food, cheeni aur maida ka zyada istemaal.
- Cigarette aur alcohol.
- Paani kam peena.
- Purani bimari (sugar, thyroid) ya koi dawa ka asar.

## Changes To Make
- Do: 7–8 ghante neend — immunity ki sabse sasti aur sabse asardar dawa.
- Do: roz 30–45 minute walk ya yoga.
- Do: roz 15–20 minute dhoop.
- Do: har meal mein protein aur rang-birangi sabzi/fal.
- Do: pehle digestion theek karein — pet saaf hona sabse zaroori kadam hai.
- Do: haath dhona, mausam badalne par garam kapde.
- Do: roz 10 minute shaant baithna ya meditation.
- Avoid: cheeni aur cold drinks — yeh immunity ko kuch ghanton ke liye dabate hain.
- Avoid: packet aur processed food.
- Avoid: cigarette aur alcohol.
- Avoid: raat ko der tak jagna.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** amla (vitamin C ka sabse achha source), nimbu aur santra, haldi,
adrak, lehsun, tulsi, giloy, kali mirch, dalchini, laung, moong dal, dahi aur
chaas (gut ke liye), ghee, badam-akhrot, kaddu ke beej (zinc), til, hari
sabziyan, gajar aur shakarkandi, anaar, mausami fal, garam paani, haldi doodh.

**Avoid:** cheeni aur mithai, cold drinks aur packet juice, maida aur bakery,
packet processed food, thanda aur baasi khana, fridge se seedha khana, zyada
fried, alcohol aur cigarette, khana skip karna.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: uthte hi halka garam paani.
- Subah: 1 amla ya amla juice, aur 5–6 bhige badam.
- Subah: 30–45 minute yoga, pranayama ya walk.
- Din mein: 15–20 minute dhoop.
- Nashta 8–9 baje: garam, taza, protein ke saath.
- Dopahar 12–2: bada meal — dal, sabzi, roti, dahi, salad.
- Shaam: tulsi-adrak ki chai ya haldi doodh.
- Raat 8 baje tak: halka garam dinner.
- Raat: haldi wala garam doodh, 10:30 tak neend.
- Mausam badalne par: khana aur halka, garam paani aur zyada.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Surya Namaskar (roz 12 round — poore sharir ke liye sabse achha),
  Bhujangasana, Setu Bandhasana, Matsyasana, Sarvangasana, Trikonasana,
  Ustrasana, Balasana, Shavasana.
- Chest kholne wale poses saans aur immunity dono ke liye achhe hain.
- Pranayama: Anulom Vilom (roz 10 minute), Kapalbhati (khali pet), Bhastrika,
  Bhramari, Ujjayi.
- Roz 10 minute meditation ya Yoga Nidra — stress immunity ka sabse bada dushman hai.
- Regularity zaroori hai: roz 20 minute, hafte mein ek din 2 ghante se behtar hai.

## Home Remedies
- Amla roz — taza, juice ya powder. Sabse asardar aur sasta.
- Haldi wala garam doodh raat ko.
- Tulsi, adrak, kali mirch aur laung ka kadha — hafte mein 2–3 baar (roz nahi,
  garmi badh jaati hai).
- Giloy ka paani — mausam badalne par.
- Lehsun ki 1–2 kali subah khali pet.
- Chyawanprash 1 chammach subah (sugar ho to bina-cheeni wala, doctor se poochein).
- Roz dhoop aur achhi neend — inka koi shortcut nahi hai.
- Koi immunity booster tablet, powder ya brand khud se lambe time tak na lein.
  Zyada kadha aur zyada giloy se acidity aur liver ki problem ho sakti hai.

## When To See A Doctor
- Baar-baar infection — saal mein 4 se zyada baar antibiotics ki zaroorat padna.
- Bukhar jo 5 din se zyada chale, ya baar-baar laut aaye.
- Bina wajah weight girna, ya raat ko paseena.
- Gale, bagal ya jaangh mein gaanth (lymph node) jo hafton se ho.
- Koi zakhm bharne mein bahut time lagna.
- Lagatar khaansi 3 hafte se zyada — TB ka test karwayein.
- Bahut thakan jo aaram se bhi na jaaye.
- Mooh mein baar-baar chhale ya safed parat.
- Chhote bachche ya buzurg mein baar-baar bimari — turant doctor.
- Test karwa lein: Hb aur CBC, sugar (HbA1c), vitamin D aur B12, thyroid.
  Baar-baar bimar padne ke peeche aksar inme se koi ek kami hoti hai.
