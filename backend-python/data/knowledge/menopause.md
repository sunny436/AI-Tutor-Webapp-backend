# Menopause (Rajonivrutti)

## What It Is
- Menopause wo samay hai jab period hamesha ke liye band ho jaate hain, aksar
  45–55 saal ki umar ke beech.
- Isse pehle ke kuch saal (perimenopause) mein hormone upar-neeche hote hain,
  isliye period irregular aur lakshan sabse zyada hote hain.
- Aam lakshan: achanak garmi lagna (hot flashes), raat mein paseena, neend na
  aana, mood ka badalna, jodon mein dard, aur skin/vaginal dryness.
- Ayurveda mein yeh vata ki umar (vata kaal) hai — isliye ilaaj ka pura zor
  garmi, tel, poshan aur routine par hota hai.

## Root Cause
- Yeh koi bimari nahi, ek natural padav hai — ovary ka estrogen banana kam hona.
- Lakshan tab zyada hote hain jab: neend puri na ho, stress zyada ho, exercise
  bilkul na ho, aur khana kamzor ho.
- Vata badhane wali aadatein: thanda-sookha khana, irregular routine, zyada travel.
- Bone ki majbooti kam hone lagti hai — calcium aur vitamin D ki kami se aur tez.
- Weight badhna aur muscle kam hona is umar mein aam hai, jo lakshan badhata hai.

## Changes To Make
- Do: roz weight-bearing exercise — walk, halki strength training. Yeh haddiyon
  ke liye sabse zaroori hai.
- Do: calcium, protein aur vitamin D wala khana roz.
- Do: roz 15–20 minute dhoop.
- Do: sone-uthne ka time fix karein.
- Do: hot flash ke liye kapde ki layers pehnein, kamra thanda rakhein.
- Do: apne triggers pehchanein — aksar teekha khana, garam drink, alcohol aur stress.
- Avoid: cigarette aur alcohol — dono hot flashes aur haddi dono bigadte hain.
- Avoid: bahut teekha aur garam khana agar hot flashes zyada hon.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** til aur alsi (natural phyto-estrogen), soya, ghee, doodh-dahi-paneer,
badam aur akhrot, khajoor, hari sabziyan, ragi (calcium ke liye), moong dal,
anaar, nariyal paani, saunf, dhaniya, shatavari wala doodh, garam pakaya hua khana.

**Avoid:** alcohol aur cigarette, bahut teekha aur masaledar khana, zyada
chai/coffee (neend aur hot flashes dono bigadte hain), packet aur processed food,
zyada namak (haddi se calcium kheenchta hai), zyada cheeni aur maida.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: fix time par uthein, halka garam paani.
- Subah: 20–30 minute walk ya halki strength training.
- Subah: 20 minute yoga aur pranayama.
- Din mein: 15–20 minute dhoop.
- Dopahar: bada meal 12–2 ke beech, protein aur calcium ke saath.
- Shaam: nuts, khajoor ya til-gud ka chhota snack.
- Raat: halka garam dinner 8 baje tak, kamra thanda rakhein.
- Raat: pairon aur sar ki til tel maalish (Abhyanga), 10:30 tak neend.
- Hafte mein 2–3 baar poore sharir ki tel maalish — vata ke liye sabse achhi.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Setu Bandhasana, Viparita Karani, Supta Baddha Konasana, Balasana,
  Bhujangasana, Tadasana, Vrikshasana (balance ke liye), Marjariasana, Shavasana.
- Weight-bearing poses (Tadasana, Virabhadrasana) haddiyon ke liye khaas achhe hain.
- Pranayama: Shitali aur Sheetkari (hot flashes ke liye seedha thandak),
  Bhramari (mood aur neend), Anulom Vilom, Chandra Bhedana.
- Roz 10 minute Yoga Nidra ya meditation.
- Hot flash ke waqt turant: Shitali pranayama 10 baar.

## Home Remedies
- Shatavari ya til garam doodh ke saath — Ayurveda mein is umar ke liye khaas.
- Roz 1 chammach alsi ya til powder.
- Hot flash ke waqt Shitali pranayama aur thanda paani ka sip.
- Til tel se poore sharir ki maalish, hafte mein 2–3 baar.
- Saunf ka paani din bhar.
- Roz dhoop aur walk.
- Koi hormone tablet, supplement brand ya dosage khud se na lein — HRT ka faisla
  sirf doctor karta hai.

## When To See A Doctor
- Menopause ke baad koi bhi bleeding — chahe ek boond hi ho. Yeh kabhi normal
  nahi hoti, turant doctor ko dikhayein.
- Bahut zyada bleeding ya bade clots perimenopause mein.
- Hot flashes ya neend ki problem itni ki roz ka jeevan ruk jaaye.
- 40 saal se pehle period band ho jaayein.
- Chest pain, saans phoolna, ya bahut tez dil dhadakna.
- Haddi ka fracture halki chot se hi ho jaana — bone density test karwayein.
- Lagatar udasi, ghabrahat ya jeene ka mann na hona.
- Is umar mein regular check-up zaroori hai: thyroid, sugar, BP, cholesterol,
  bone density aur breast/cervical screening.
