# Weight Gain & Obesity (Sthaulya)

## What It Is
- Sthaulya mein kapha dosha aur "meda dhatu" (body fat) badh jaate hain, aur
  digestion ki aag (agni) dheemi pad jaati hai.
- Ayurveda ka nazariya: yeh sirf zyada khane ka nateeja nahi hai — dheemi agni
  aur inactive lifestyle isse banaye rakhte hain.
- Isliye ilaaj mein khana kam karna nahi, balki agni tez karna aur movement
  badhana pehle aata hai.

## Root Cause
- Din bhar baithe rehna aur bilkul physical activity na hona.
- Refined carbs: cheeni, maida, cold drinks, bakery, packet snacks.
- Bina bhookh ke khana aur baar-baar snacking.
- Raat ko late aur bhaari dinner, phir turant so jaana.
- Neend puri na hona — bhookh ke hormone seedha bigadte hain.
- Stress — cortisol pet par fat jama karta hai.
- Din mein sona, khaas kar khane ke baad (Ayurveda mein kapha badhane ka sabse
  bada karan).
- Thyroid ki kami, PCOS, ya kuch dawaiyan bhi wajah ho sakti hain.

## Changes To Make
- Do: roz 45 minute movement — brisk walk, strength training ya yoga.
- Do: sabse bada meal dopahar mein, dinner sabse halka.
- Do: har meal mein protein aur fibre — bhookh apne aap control hoti hai.
- Do: 7–8 ghante neend.
- Do: dheere-dheere target rakhein — mahine mein 2–3 kg se zyada nahi.
- Avoid: cheeni, cold drink, packet juice, maida, fried aur fast food.
- Avoid: khane ke baad din mein sona.
- Avoid: crash diet aur bhookhe rehna — agni aur kamzor hoti hai, weight wapas
  aata hai.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** moong dal, jau (barley), bajra, ragi, oats, hari sabziyan, lauki,
tori, karela, methi, salad khane se pehle, chaas, paneer, anda, machhli,
alsi aur kaddu ke beej, amla, adrak, kali mirch, dalchini, haldi, jeera-dhaniya
paani, halka garam paani din bhar.

**Avoid:** cheeni aur mithai, cold drinks aur packet juice, maida, white bread,
biscuit-cake, fried aur fast food, zyada chawal aur aloo, cheese aur bahut
zyada dairy, alcohol, packet snacks, raat ko heavy khana.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: uthte hi halka garam paani, nimbu ya shahad ke saath.
- Subah: 45 minute exercise — khali pet walk sabse achhi.
- Nashta: protein wala (moong chilla, anda, paneer) — bread/cereal nahi.
- Dopahar 12–2: sabse bada meal, salad pehle khaayein.
- Shaam: chaas ya nuts ka chhota snack, chai bina cheeni.
- Raat: sabse halka dinner 7–8 baje tak — soup, sabzi, dal.
- Dinner ke baad: 15 minute walk, phir 2 ghante baad hi sona.
- Din mein sona nahi.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Surya Namaskar (roz 12 round, dheere badhayein), Trikonasana,
  Virabhadrasana, Utkatasana (chair pose), Naukasana, Dhanurasana,
  Bhujangasana, Plank, Setu Bandhasana.
- Kapha ke liye tez aur flowing practice chahiye — dheemi restorative nahi.
- Pranayama: Kapalbhati (khali pet, roz badhayein), Bhastrika, Anulom Vilom,
  Agnisar kriya.
- Roz kam se kam 30–45 minute. Consistency se hi kaam banta hai.
- Ghutne ya kamar ki problem ho to pehle trainer ya doctor se poochein.

## Home Remedies
- Subah khali pet halka garam paani nimbu ke saath.
- Methi ke daane raat bhar bhigo kar subah.
- Jeera-dhaniya-saunf ka paani din bhar sip karna.
- Khane se pehle salad ya soup — pet pehle bharta hai.
- Dalchini ka paani.
- Amla juice ya taza amla roz.
- Roz walk — koi bhi "fat cutter" isse zyada kaam nahi karta.
- Koi weight-loss tablet, powder, tea ya brand khud se na lein — yeh nuksaan
  karte hain.

## When To See A Doctor
- Weight bina koshish ke tezi se badh raha ho.
- Saath mein bahut thakan, thand lagna, aur baal jhadna — thyroid test karwayein.
- Ladies mein weight ke saath irregular period aur chehre par baal — PCOS test.
- Gardan ya bagal ki skin kaali padna (insulin resistance ka sign).
- Kharraate ke saath raat mein saans ruk jaana.
- Chest pain, saans phoolna, ya pairon mein sujan.
- Ghutne ya kamar ka dard jo exercise rok raha ho.
- Weight ke saath sugar, BP aur cholesterol ka test zaroor karwa lein — inka
  proper ilaaj doctor hi karta hai.
