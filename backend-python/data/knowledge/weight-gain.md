# Underweight & Healthy Weight Gain (Karshya)

## What It Is
- Karshya mein sharir ke dhatu (tissue) kam ban paate hain — body dubli, kamzor
  aur jaldi thakne wali ho jaati hai.
- Ayurveda mein iski jad badha hua vata aur kamzor absorption hai: khana jaata
  toh hai, par body use lagta nahi.
- Isliye sirf "zyada khao" kaam nahi karta — pehle digestion aur absorption
  theek karna padta hai.

## Root Cause
- Kamzor digestion — khaya hua khana theek se absorb nahi hota.
- Khana skip karna aur meal timing bilkul unfix hona.
- Stress, chinta aur neend ki kami — vata seedha badhta hai.
- Bahut zyada physical kaam ya exercise, khane ke hisaab se.
- Sookha, thanda aur halka khana hi khate rehna.
- Pet ke keede (worms), khaas kar bachchon mein.
- Thyroid ka tez hona, diabetes, TB ya koi purani bimari — inki jaanch zaroori hai.
- Bina wajah weight girna hamesha doctor se check karwana chahiye.

## Changes To Make
- Do: har 3 ghante mein kuch khaayein — 3 meals + 2–3 snacks.
- Do: khane ki quality badhayein, sirf quantity nahi — ghee, nuts, doodh, dal.
- Do: strength training karein — fat nahi, muscle banana hai.
- Do: khana fix time par, shaanti se baith kar.
- Do: 7–8 ghante neend — muscle neend mein banti hai.
- Avoid: khana skip karna aur bahut lamba gap.
- Avoid: khane se pehle chai/coffee — bhookh mar jaati hai.
- Avoid: bahut zyada cardio aur running.
- Avoid: junk food se weight badhana — yeh sirf fat aur bimari deta hai.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** ghee (har meal mein), doodh aur paneer, kela, khajoor, anjeer, kishmish,
badam-akhrot-kaju, moong aur urad dal, chawal, gehun, oats, aloo, shakarkandi,
anda, paneer, nariyal, til-gud ke laddu, banana shake, ashwagandha wala doodh.

**Avoid:** khana skip karna, sirf salad aur halka khana, bahut zyada chai/coffee,
cigarette, cold drinks aur junk food (weight fat ke roop mein badhta hai, sehat
nahi banti), packet processed food.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: uthte hi bhige badam aur khajoor.
- Subah: 30–40 minute strength training ya yoga (cardio kam).
- Nashta 8–9 baje: bhaari aur poshak — paratha ghee ke saath, poha, anda, doodh.
- 11 baje: banana shake ya nuts.
- Dopahar 12–2: bada meal — dal, chawal, sabzi, roti ghee ke saath, dahi.
- 4–5 baje: khajoor, laddu ya paneer ka snack.
- Raat 8 baje: poora dinner, ghee ke saath.
- Sone se pehle: garam doodh mein ghee ya khajoor.
- Roz til tel se poore sharir ki maalish (Abhyanga) — vata ke liye sabse achhi.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Surya Namaskar (dheere, strength ke liye), Bhujangasana, Dhanurasana,
  Setu Bandhasana, Vajrasana (khane ke baad), Balasana, Shavasana,
  Sarvangasana (agar comfortable ho).
- Strength training (weights, push-ups, squats) yoga ke saath zaroor karein —
  muscle isi se banti hai.
- Pranayama: Anulom Vilom, Bhramari, slow deep belly breathing.
- Avoid: bahut zyada Kapalbhati aur Bhastrika, lambi running aur zyada cardio —
  yeh calorie jalate hain aur vata badhate hain.

## Home Remedies
- Ashwagandha wala garam doodh raat ko — Ayurveda mein taakat ke liye khaas.
- Roz 5–6 bhige badam aur 2 khajoor subah khali pet.
- Kela + doodh + khajoor ka shake.
- Har meal mein 1 chammach ghee.
- Til aur gud ke laddu.
- Kishmish aur anjeer raat bhar bhigo kar subah.
- Roz til tel ki poore sharir maalish.
- Koi weight-gain powder, protein brand ya steroid khud se na lein — steroid se
  weight badhta hai par liver aur hormone barbaad ho jaate hain.

## When To See A Doctor
- Bina koshish ke weight tezi se kam ho raha ho — yeh hamesha check karwana chahiye.
- Weight kam hone ke saath bukhar, raat ko paseena, ya lagatar khaansi (TB ki jaanch).
- Bahut bhookh lagne ke bawajood weight girna, saath mein pyaas aur baar-baar
  urine (diabetes ki jaanch).
- Dil tez dhadakna, haath kaapna aur garmi lagna (thyroid tez hone ka sign).
- Lagatar loose motions ya pet dard.
- Khoon ki kami ke signs: bahut peela dikhna, chakkar, saans phoolna.
- Khana khane se dar lagna ya jaan bujh kar na khana — iske liye proper madad lein.
- Thyroid, sugar, khoon ki kami, vitamin B12 aur celiac ka test karwa lein.
