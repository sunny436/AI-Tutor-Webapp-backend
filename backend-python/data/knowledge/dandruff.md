# Dandruff (Darunaka)

## What It Is
- Dandruff mein sar ki skin se safed ya peeli papdi girti hai, saath mein khujli
  aur kabhi kabhi jalan hoti hai.
- Iske peeche aksar ek natural fungus (Malassezia) hota hai jo sar par extra tel
  milne par badh jata hai.
- Ayurveda mein isko "darunaka" kehte hain — kapha aur vata ka sar ki skin par
  bigadna, jisse sookhapan aur papdi dono aate hain.

## Root Cause
- Sar par fungus ka badh jaana, khaas kar tailiya scalp par.
- Sar theek se saaf na hona, ya shampoo achhe se na dhulna.
- Bahut zyada shampoo karna — natural tel nikal jaata hai aur sookhapan badhta hai.
- Bahut garam paani se baal dhona.
- Sardi ka mausam aur sookhi hawa.
- Stress aur neend ki kami.
- Bahut teekha, tala hua aur meetha khana.
- Chemical hair products aur baar-baar colour.
- Tel lagane ke baad kai din tak na dhona — fungus ko khana milta hai.

## Changes To Make
- Do: hafte mein 2–3 baar baal dhoyein — na bahut kam, na roz.
- Do: shampoo ko sar par achhe se ragad kar 3–5 minute chhodein, phir dhoyein.
- Do: normal ya halke thande paani se baal dhoyein.
- Do: kanghi, takiya cover aur towel saaf rakhein, alag rakhein.
- Do: tel lagane ke 1–2 ghante baad dho lein, kai din na chhodein.
- Avoid: bahut garam paani.
- Avoid: sar khujlana — isse skin chhil jaati hai aur infection badhta hai.
- Avoid: bahut zyada hair gel, spray aur chemical product.
- Avoid: bahut teekha, meetha aur tala hua khana.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** dahi aur chaas, hari sabziyan, moong dal, amla, nariyal, akhrot aur
alsi (omega-3 ke liye), kaddu ke beej (zinc ke liye), anda, taza fal, haldi,
neem ke patte (thodi matra), khoob paani.

**Avoid:** bahut teekha aur masaledar khana, tala hua, zyada cheeni aur mithai,
maida aur bakery, packet processed food, zyada dairy agar dandruff badhe,
alcohol, khamir (yeast) wali cheezein zyada.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: halka garam paani, amla ya taza fal.
- Subah: 20 minute yoga aur pranayama.
- Din bhar: khoob paani.
- Hafte mein 2–3 baar: baal dhona, shampoo ko 3–5 minute chhod kar.
- Baal dhone se 1–2 ghante pehle: nariyal tel ki halki maalish (raat bhar nahi).
- Raat: halka dinner 8 baje tak, kam teekha.
- Raat: takiya cover hafte mein badlein.
- 10:30 tak neend — stress dandruff ka bada trigger hai.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Adho Mukha Svanasana, Uttanasana, Balasana, Sarvangasana,
  Viparita Karani, Setu Bandhasana, Marjariasana, Shavasana.
- Sar tak blood flow badhane wale poses skin ki sehat sudharte hain.
- Pranayama: Anulom Vilom, Bhramari, Shitali aur Sheetkari (garmi aur khujli ke
  liye), Kapalbhati (khali pet).
- Roz 10 minute meditation — stress se dandruff seedha badhta hai.
- High BP ya gardan ki problem ho to inversions se pehle doctor se poochein.

## Home Remedies
- Nariyal tel mein kuch neem ke patte ubaal kar, thanda karke sar par 1 ghanta,
  phir dho lein.
- Nimbu ka ras nariyal tel mein mila kar 20 minute (khujli ho to na karein).
- Dahi sar par 20–30 minute, phir dho lein.
- Methi ke daane raat bhar bhigo kar peeskar paste, 30 minute.
- Aloe vera gel — khujli aur jalan dono ke liye.
- Neem ke patte ubaal kar us paani se aakhri baar baal dhona.
- Tulsi ya neem ka paani sar dhone mein.
- Koi anti-dandruff medicine ya steroid lotion khud se na lagayein — halke
  anti-dandruff shampoo theek hain, par baar-baar na sudhre to doctor se milein.

## When To See A Doctor
- Sar par laal, sooji hui skin, mawad ya badbu — infection ho sakta hai.
- Motti peeli-chikni papdi ya sar par ubhre hue laal chakatte (seborrheic
  dermatitis ya psoriasis ho sakta hai).
- Dandruff ke saath baal tezi se jhadna ya khaali patch banna.
- Bahut tez khujli jo neend kharab kar de.
- Papdi kaan, bhaunh, naak ke aas-paas ya chehre par bhi phail jaaye.
- 4–6 hafte ghar ke upay aur anti-dandruff shampoo se bhi na sudhre.
- Bachche ke sar par ho aur badh raha ho — doctor ko dikhayein.
