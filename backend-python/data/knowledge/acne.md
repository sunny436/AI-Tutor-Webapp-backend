# Acne & Pimples (Yuvan Pidika)

## What It Is
- Muhase tab hote hain jab skin ke chhote chhed (pores) tel aur mari hui skin se
  band ho jaate hain, phir usme bacteria badh jaate hain.
- Ayurveda mein isko badha hua pitta (garmi) aur kapha (tel) maana jata hai,
  jise kharab digestion aur "ama" aur bigadta hai.
- Sabse zaroori baat: muhase andar ki halat ka bahar dikhne wala nishan hain —
  sirf cream se poori tarah theek nahi hote.

## Root Cause
- Hormone ka badalna — teenage, period se pehle, ya PCOS.
- Zyada meetha, maida aur cold drinks — yeh insulin badha kar acne badhate hain.
- Zyada dairy, khaas kar doodh.
- Tala hua aur bahut teekha khana — pitta badhata hai.
- Kabz aur kharab digestion.
- Stress aur neend ki kami.
- Chehre par bhaari makeup, tailiya cream, aur raat ko makeup na hatana.
- Baar-baar chehra choona aur pimple phodna.
- Gandi takiya cover, phone screen aur towel.
- Kuch dawaiyan aur steroid cream.

## Changes To Make
- Do: chehra din mein 2 baar halke face wash se dhoyein — isse zyada nahi.
- Do: takiya cover hafte mein badlein, phone screen roz saaf karein.
- Do: raat ko makeup poori tarah hataayein.
- Do: kabz ko theek rakhein — pet saaf hona skin par seedha dikhta hai.
- Do: sunscreen aur oil-free (non-comedogenic) product istemaal karein.
- Do: 7–8 ghante neend.
- Avoid: pimple phodna ya nochna — nishan (scar) yahin se banta hai.
- Avoid: baar-baar chehra choona.
- Avoid: bhaari tailiya cream aur thick makeup.
- Avoid: steroid cream — yeh pehle theek dikhati hai phir acne bahut bigad jaata hai.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** hari sabziyan, lauki, kheera, moong dal, amla, anaar, papita, nariyal
paani, chaas, haldi, dhaniya, saunf, alsi aur kaddu ke beej, akhrot, taza fal,
khoob paani (8–10 glass), neem aur tulsi ki halki chai.

**Avoid:** cheeni, mithai, chocolate, cold drinks, packet juice, maida, white
bread, biscuit-cake, doodh zyada, cheese aur paneer zyada, tala hua aur fast
food, bahut teekha aur masaledar, achaar, packet processed food, whey protein
powder, alcohol aur cigarette.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: halka garam paani, phir amla ya nariyal paani.
- Subah: chehra halke face wash se dhoyein, phir oil-free moisturiser aur sunscreen.
- Subah: 20–30 minute yoga ya walk — paseena skin ke liye achha hai.
- Nashta: halka aur taza.
- Dopahar 12–2: bada meal — dal, sabzi, salad.
- Din bhar: 8–10 glass paani.
- Shaam: gym/walk ke turant baad chehra dhoyein.
- Raat: makeup hataayein, chehra dhoyein, halka moisturiser.
- Raat: halka dinner 8 baje tak, 10:30 tak neend.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Sarvangasana, Halasana, Adho Mukha Svanasana, Uttanasana,
  Bhujangasana, Matsyasana, Trikonasana, Setu Bandhasana, Shavasana.
- Chehre tak blood flow badhane wale poses skin ki repair tez karte hain.
- Pranayama: Shitali aur Sheetkari (pitta ki garmi seedha nikalte hain),
  Anulom Vilom, Bhramari, Kapalbhati (khali pet).
- Simhasana (lion pose) — chehre ke liye khaas.
- Roz 10 minute meditation — stress hormone acne ka bada driver hai.

## Home Remedies
- Haldi aur besan ka paste chandan ke saath, 15 minute, phir dho lein.
- Neem ke patte ubaal kar us paani se chehra dhona.
- Aloe vera gel — jalan aur laali dono ke liye.
- Multani mitti ka paste gulab jal ke saath, hafte mein 1–2 baar (roz nahi,
  skin sookh jaati hai).
- Amla juice ya taza amla roz.
- Dhaniya aur saunf ka paani din bhar.
- Nariyal paani roz.
- Toothpaste, nimbu ya baking soda chehre par bilkul na lagayein — yeh skin jala
  dete hain aur nishan chhod jaate hain.
- Koi acne tablet, antibiotic ya steroid cream khud se na lein.

## When To See A Doctor
- Bade, dard karne wale, gaanth jaise muhase (cystic acne) — yeh nishan chhod
  dete hain, jitni jaldi ilaaj ho utna achha.
- Muhase se nishan aur gadde ban rahe hon.
- Chehre par tezi se failne wale daane, ya mawad bhare.
- Ladies mein acne ke saath irregular period aur chehre par baal — PCOS test.
- Acne 2–3 mahine ghar ke upay se bhi na sudhre.
- Achanak acne shuru ho jaaye badi umar mein.
- Bukhar ya sujan ke saath muhase.
- Muhase se mann bahut kharab ho raha ho ya bahar jaana band ho gaya ho — yeh
  bhi doctor ke paas jaane ki poori wajah hai.
