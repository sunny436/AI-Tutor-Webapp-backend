# Irregular Periods (Artava Dushti)

## What It Is
- Normal cycle 21 se 35 din ka hota hai. Isse baar-baar chhota, bada ya bilkul
  unpredictable hona irregular period kehlata hai.
- Ayurveda mein period ko "artava" kehte hain, aur iska time vata dosha
  chalata hai — isliye irregular routine seedha period ko chhoo jata hai.
- Wajah aksar hormonal hoti hai: PCOS, thyroid, weight ka badalna, stress ya
  bahut zyada exercise.

## Root Cause
- Vata badhane wali lifestyle: irregular sona, khana aur bahut travel.
- Weight ka bahut kam ya bahut zyada hona.
- Lagatar stress aur neend ki kami.
- Crash diet, bhookhe rehna, ya bahut zyada intense exercise.
- PCOS ya thyroid ki problem.
- Khoon ki kami (anaemia) aur poshan ki kami.
- Delivery ke baad ya breastfeeding ka time.
- Menopause ke aas-paas ki umar (40 ke baad).

## Changes To Make
- Do: khana, sona aur uthna — teenon ka time fix karein. Vata ke liye yahi sabse
  badi dawa hai.
- Do: roz 30 minute halka movement, walk ya yoga.
- Do: weight ko healthy range mein laayein, dheere-dheere.
- Do: iron aur protein wala khana roz.
- Do: cycle ki date ek diary ya app mein note karein.
- Avoid: crash diet, bhookhe rehna aur bahut zyada intense workout.
- Avoid: raat ko der tak jagna.
- Avoid: bahut thanda, sookha aur baasi khana — vata badhata hai.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** til (khaas kar kaale til), gud, khajoor, anjeer, kishmish, palak aur
hari sabziyan, chukandar, anaar, papita, moong dal, ghee, badam-akhrot, alsi
aur kaddu ke beej, ajwain, saunf, haldi wala doodh, garam pakaya hua khana.

**Avoid:** thanda aur baasi khana, packet aur processed food, zyada cheeni aur
maida, bahut zyada chai/coffee, cold drinks, alcohol, khana skip karna,
bahut kam calorie wali diet.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: halka garam paani, phir 30 minute yoga ya walk.
- Nashta: time par, protein ke saath.
- Dopahar: bada meal 12–2 ke beech, dal-sabzi-roti aur salad.
- Shaam: khajoor, til-gud ka laddu ya nuts ka chhota snack.
- Raat: halka garam dinner 8 baje tak.
- Raat: pairon ki til tel se maalish, 10:30 tak neend.
- Period se 1 hafta pehle: khana aur bhi halka aur garam rakhein, aaram zyada.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Baddha Konasana (butterfly), Supta Baddha Konasana, Malasana,
  Bhujangasana, Dhanurasana, Setu Bandhasana, Ustrasana, Balasana,
  Viparita Karani.
- Surya Namaskar roz 6–12 round — poore hormone system ko balance karta hai.
- Pranayama: Anulom Vilom, Bhramari, Kapalbhati (sirf khali pet aur period ke
  dino ke alawa).
- Roz 10 minute meditation — stress cycle ka bada karan hai.
- Avoid: period ke bhaari bleeding wale dino mein inversions (Sirsasana,
  Sarvangasana) aur pet par dabav.

## Home Remedies
- Kaale til aur gud ka laddu — period se 1 hafta pehle roz ek.
- Ajwain ya saunf ka garam paani.
- Papita — cycle ko regular karne mein madadgar mana jata hai.
- Anaar aur chukandar ka juice — khoon banane ke liye.
- Haldi wala garam doodh raat ko.
- Roz pairon aur pet ki halki til tel maalish.
- Koi hormone tablet, period lane wali dawa ya brand khud se na lein — yeh
  khatarnak ho sakta hai.

## When To See A Doctor
- Period 3 mahine se zyada band ho jaayein (aur pregnancy na ho).
- Bahut zyada bleeding — har 1–2 ghante mein pad badalna pade, ya bade clots.
- Bleeding 7 din se zyada chale.
- Do periods ke beech mein spotting ya bleeding.
- Sex ke baad bleeding.
- Bahut tez pelvic dard.
- Cycle achanak bilkul badal jaaye, khaas kar 40 ki umar ke baad.
- Bachche ke liye 1 saal se koshish kar rahi hon.
- Thyroid, khoon ki kami aur PCOS ka test zaroor karwayein — teenon hi irregular
  periods ki aam wajah hain aur inka proper ilaaj hota hai.
