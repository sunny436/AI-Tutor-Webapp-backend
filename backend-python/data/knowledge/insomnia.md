# Insomnia & Poor Sleep (Anidra)

## What It Is
- Anidra mein ya to neend aati hi nahi, ya baar-baar khul jaati hai, ya subah
  uthne par taazgi nahi lagti.
- Ayurveda mein neend ka na aana mukhya roop se badhe hue vata (mann ka na rukna)
  aur kabhi pitta (garmi aur gussa) ki wajah se hota hai.
- Achhi neend Ayurveda mein sehat ke teen stambh (aahar, nidra, brahmacharya)
  mein se ek hai — isliye isko theek karna baaki har cheez ko theek karta hai.

## Root Cause
- Sone aur uthne ka time roz badalna.
- Sone se pehle phone, TV aur laptop ki roshni.
- Shaam ke baad chai, coffee ya energy drink.
- Raat ko bahut late, bhari ya bahut teekha khana.
- Stress, overthinking aur kaam ki tension bistar tak le jaana.
- Din mein lambi jhapki (khaas kar shaam ko).
- Bilkul physical activity na hona.
- Alcohol — isse neend aati zaroor hai par tooti-footi aur kam quality ki.
- Kamra bahut garam, roshan ya shor wala hona.

## Changes To Make
- Do: sone aur uthne ka ek hi time roz, weekend par bhi.
- Do: sone se 1 ghanta pehle phone aur TV band.
- Do: bistar sirf sone ke liye — usme kaam ya scrolling nahi.
- Do: kamra andhera, thanda aur shaant rakhein.
- Do: din mein 20–30 minute dhoop aur walk.
- Avoid: dopahar 3 baje ke baad chai/coffee.
- Avoid: shaam ki lambi jhapki.
- Avoid: raat ko bhari, teekha ya bahut late khana.
- Avoid: bistar par leta kar "neend kyun nahi aa rahi" sochna — 20 minute baad
  uth kar kuch shaant kaam karein, phir wapas aayein.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** halka garam doodh (haldi, jaiphal ya kesar ke saath), khichdi, ghee,
bhige badam aur akhrot, kela, khajoor, oats, garam pakayi sabzi, kaddu ke beej,
tulsi ya kaimomile jaisi bina caffeine wali chai.

**Avoid:** chai, coffee aur cola shaam ke baad, alcohol, chocolate raat ko,
bahut teekha aur tala hua dinner, packet food, bahut zyada meetha, raat ko
bhari non-veg, sone se pehle bahut zyada paani.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: fix time par uthein — neend sudharne ki shuruat subah se hoti hai.
- Subah: 15–20 minute dhoop aur walk.
- Subah: 20 minute yoga aur pranayama.
- Dopahar: bada meal 12–2 ke beech.
- Shaam 3 baje ke baad: caffeine band.
- Shaam: halki walk ya stretching.
- Raat: halka garam dinner 7:30–8 baje tak.
- Sone se 1 ghanta pehle: screen band, halki roshni.
- Sone se pehle: pairon aur sar ki til tel se maalish, garam doodh, 10:30 tak bistar.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Balasana, Viparita Karani (deewar par pair, 10 minute), Supta Baddha
  Konasana, Paschimottanasana, Setu Bandhasana, Shavasana.
- Pranayama: Bhramari (sabse asardar), Chandra Bhedana (baayein nathuna),
  Anulom Vilom, slow 4-count andar / 6-count bahar saans.
- Bistar par: 10 minute Yoga Nidra ya body scan.
- Avoid: raat ko Kapalbhati, Bhastrika aur koi bhi tez exercise — yeh jagata hai.

## Home Remedies
- Sone se pehle garam doodh mein chutki jaiphal ya haldi.
- Pairon ke talwon par til ya nariyal tel ki 5 minute maalish (Padabhyanga).
- Sar par halke haath se tel ki maalish.
- 5–6 bhige badam roz.
- Kamre mein lavender jaisi halki khushbu.
- Sone se pehle garam paani se pair dhona.
- Koi sleeping pill, tablet ya syrup khud se na lein — inki aadat pad jaati hai.

## When To See A Doctor
- Neend ki problem 3–4 hafte se zyada chal rahi ho aur in changes se na sudhre.
- Din mein itni neend ki gaadi chalate ya kaam karte waqt jhapki aa jaaye.
- Kharraate ke saath raat mein saans ruk jaana ya haanfte hue uthna — yeh sleep
  apnea ho sakta hai, iska proper test hota hai.
- Chest pain ya saans phoolne ki wajah se neend khulna.
- Neend ke saath lagatar udasi, ghabrahat ya jeene ka mann na hona.
- Kisi bhi sleeping medicine par nirbharta ban gayi ho.
- Thyroid aur khoon ki kami ka test karwa lein — yeh bhi neend bigadte hain.
