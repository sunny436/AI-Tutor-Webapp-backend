# Dry Skin & Skin Glow (Rukshata / Twacha Kanti)

## What It Is
- Rukhi skin mein twacha ka natural tel aur nami kam ho jaati hai — skin kheenchti
  hai, papdi banti hai, aur chehre ki chamak chali jaati hai.
- Ayurveda mein iski jad badha hua vata (sookhapan) aur kamzor "rasa dhatu"
  (body ka poshan) hai.
- Asli chamak bahar ki cream se nahi, andar ke poshan, achhi neend aur saaf
  digestion se aati hai.

## Root Cause
- Paani kam peena.
- Khane mein achhe fat (ghee, tel, nuts) ki kami — crash diet aur fat-free diet.
- Sardi ka mausam, sookhi hawa aur AC mein zyada rehna.
- Bahut garam paani se nahana aur lamba nahana.
- Kadwa soap aur bahut zyada face wash — natural tel nikal jaata hai.
- Neend puri na hona aur lagatar stress.
- Dhoop mein bina bachav ke rehna.
- Kabz aur kharab digestion — chehra seedha dull dikhta hai.
- Khoon ki kami, thyroid ki kami aur vitamin D/B12 ki kami.
- Cigarette aur alcohol.

## Changes To Make
- Do: din mein 8–10 glass paani.
- Do: khane mein ghee, nuts aur seeds shaamil karein — skin ko andar se tel chahiye.
- Do: nahane ke turant baad, geeli skin par hi tel ya moisturiser lagayein.
- Do: halke thande ya gungune paani se nahayein, 10 minute se kam.
- Do: roz sunscreen, chahe ghar mein hi hon.
- Do: 7–8 ghante neend — skin raat mein hi repair hoti hai.
- Avoid: bahut garam paani aur lamba nahana.
- Avoid: kadwa soap aur baar-baar face wash.
- Avoid: fat-free ya crash diet.
- Avoid: cigarette aur alcohol — dono skin ko sabse jaldi budha karte hain.

## Food Chart
**Eat:** ghee (roz 1–2 chammach), badam-akhrot, alsi aur kaddu ke beej, til,
nariyal aur nariyal tel, avocado, doodh, dahi, khajoor aur kishmish, amla (roz),
anaar, papita, gajar, chukandar, kheera, hari sabziyan, moong dal, shakarkandi,
haldi, khoob paani, nariyal paani.

**Avoid:** paani kam peena, fat-free aur crash diet, packet aur processed food,
zyada cheeni aur maida, bahut zyada chai/coffee (paani sukhate hain), alcohol,
cigarette, cold drinks, bahut namkeen aur sookha khana.

## Daily Routine
- Subah: halka garam paani, phir bhige badam aur amla.
- Subah: 20–30 minute yoga ya walk — paseena skin saaf karta hai.
- Nahane se 15 minute pehle: til ya nariyal tel se poore sharir ki maalish
  (Abhyanga) — vata aur sookhi skin ke liye Ayurveda ka sabse bada upay.
- Nahane ke baad: geeli skin par moisturiser ya tel.
- Din: sunscreen, aur 8–10 glass paani.
- Dopahar 12–2: bada meal, ghee aur salad ke saath.
- Shaam: nuts aur seeds ka snack.
- Raat: halka dinner 8 baje tak.
- Raat: chehra dhoyein, halka tel/moisturiser, pairon par tel; 10:30 tak neend.

## Yoga & Breathing
- Asanas: Sarvangasana, Halasana, Adho Mukha Svanasana, Uttanasana, Matsyasana,
  Bhujangasana, Trikonasana, Setu Bandhasana, Viparita Karani, Shavasana.
- Aage jhukne aur ulte hone wale poses chehre tak khoon pahunchate hain — yahi
  natural glow deta hai.
- Simhasana (lion pose) — chehre ki muscles ke liye.
- Pranayama: Anulom Vilom, Bhramari, Shitali (skin ko thandak), Kapalbhati
  (khali pet, detox ke liye).
- Roz 10 minute meditation — neend aur skin dono sudharte hain.
- High BP ya gardan ki problem ho to inversions se pehle doctor se poochein.

## Home Remedies
- Roz til ya nariyal tel se poore sharir ki maalish, nahane se pehle.
- Malai ya dahi mein haldi mila kar chehre par 15 minute.
- Aloe vera gel raat ko chehre par.
- Besan + haldi + doodh ka paste, hafte mein 1–2 baar.
- Amla roz — Ayurveda mein skin aur baal dono ke liye sabse khaas.
- Gulab jal se chehra pochna.
- Nariyal paani aur khoob saada paani.
- Nimbu, toothpaste ya baking soda chehre par na lagayein — skin jal jaati hai.
- Koi steroid cream ya "instant fairness" cream na lagayein — yeh skin
  permanently patli aur kharab kar deti hain.

## When To See A Doctor
- Skin par laal, sooje hue, khujli wale chakatte jo failte ja rahe hon (eczema
  ya psoriasis ho sakta hai).
- Skin phatna, khoon aana ya mawad.
- Bahut tez khujli jo neend kharab kar de.
- Rukhi skin ke saath bahut thakan, thand lagna aur baal jhadna — thyroid test.
- Skin ka rang achanak badalna, ya koi til/mole ka size, rang ya shape badalna.
- Achanak poore sharir par daane ya khujli — allergy ho sakti hai.
- Bahut peela ya peeli aankhein dikhna.
- Test karwa lein: thyroid, khoon ki kami, sugar aur vitamin D/B12 — inki kami
  se skin sabse pehle kharab hoti hai.
